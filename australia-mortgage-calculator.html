Australian Mortgage Planning

Page revision 2026-10-26 · Scenario estimates; confirm actual lender and transaction terms.

Keep the inputs distinct

The loan starts with price minus deposit. If a premium is financed, principal is larger. The LMI field requires an actual all-in lender quote, including applicable charges once. Enter zero to explicitly exclude a charge. A quote is not a promise that a particular guarantee applies.

A monthly P&I illustration divides the annual percentage by twelve. Actual Australian products commonly accrue interest daily, so payment dates, fees, offset balances and rounding matter. At AUD 520,000, an assumed 6.19% and 25 years, the monthly model gives AUD 3,411.02.

Transfer duty needs the correct category

Without an entered assessment, the root tool budgets published general residential schedules for all eight states/territories. NSW uses 2026/27 thresholds and premium duty for a wholly residential property of no more than two hectares. ACT uses the non-owner-occupier base schedule; other owner-occupier and first-home concessions are not inferred. The state duty tool publishes the source schedules and confirmed adjustment inputs.

For a concession or special category, enter an official assessment or conveyancer’s quote. A blank concession assessment is shown as not estimated. An entered quote overrides the general calculation; check its transaction date, legal dutiable value and applicable foreign surcharges.

Offset savings remain a scenario

A constant offset balance can reduce interest on an eligible product, but the actual benefit follows daily balances and product terms. This comparison excludes extra product fees and assumes an unchanged rate and offset balance. It is not a tax calculation or a guaranteed lifetime saving. Read Moneysmart's offset explanation.

Lending and guarantee eligibility are separate

APRA's serviceability and high-DTI settings apply within their prudential scope; they are not approval rules that this calculator can award. The DTI guide explains the lender-flow distinction.

The 5% Deposit Scheme still has eligibility and location-based price limits. Confirm support with a participating lender before assuming LMI is waived. Use the LMI guide to prepare the questions.

Australian Mortgage Frequently Asked Questions

  • LMI protects the lender and is separate from homeowners insurance. Whether it is required, its price and whether it can be financed depend on the product, lender, insurer and any confirmed scheme. Use an actual all-in quote. This site does not generate an insurer tariff solely from a deposit percentage.

  • An offset can reduce the balance used for interest under the product's terms while the contractual principal remains outstanding. Its benefit depends on the offset percentage, actual cash path, fees and interest convention. Money in an offset is not automatically principal repaid; use the offset tool instead of counting the same funds as both a larger deposit and an offset balance.

  • Compare regular repayment frequency with accelerated repayment separately. The annual amount paid, not only the size of one debit, determines the effect on debt. Confirm the lender's convention and any limits or costs on additional payments before treating an accelerated schedule as available.

  • Purchase duty and concessions depend on the state or territory, transaction date, value, property and buyer category. No single national duty range or first-home exemption is assumed. Use the supported general-rate tool and the relevant revenue authority, and enter a confirmed assessment for concessions or special cases.

  • Government deposit-support schemes have current eligibility and property rules that must be confirmed with the program and participating lender. A calculator cannot award a guarantee or determine that LMI is waived. Count only confirmed assistance and preserve a separate closing-cost reserve.

  • A serviceability assessment and a monthly repayment are different calculations. Lenders consider income, living costs, other debts and their applicable assessment policy. The RBA policy rate is not your mortgage quote. Use the actual quoted rate and a separately labelled assessment scenario without treating the result as approval.
