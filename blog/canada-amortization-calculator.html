Canada Amortization: Monthly and Accelerated Biweekly Payments

Compare Canadian monthly and accelerated biweekly payments, including the extra annual cash required and the limits of a constant-rate model.

Publisher contact: Abdul RaheemPublisher: REO TechnologiesUpdated: October 25, 2026

Paying half a monthly payment every two weeks can shorten a mortgage, but it is not free. Twenty-six half-payments equal thirteen monthly payments a year. Compared with twelve monthly payments, the scheduled annual cash commitment increases by 8.33%.

On CAD 520,000 at an assumed 3.99%, semiannual compounding and 25-year amortization, the monthly baseline is CAD 2,732.49. Half is about CAD 1,366.24 before payment rounding. The accelerated plan commits approximately one extra baseline payment each year.

Know which biweekly option you are offered

A non-accelerated biweekly amount may be set to keep the annual payment total close to the monthly arrangement. Earlier principal reduction can still affect interest slightly; the large acceleration effect comes from paying more during the year. Do not treat every option labeled biweekly as identical.

The FCAC guidance on paying a mortgage faster explains payment-frequency and prepayment choices. Confirm the actual amount and dates with your lender, including rounding and any allowance restrictions.

What this comparison calculates

The model converts the semiannual quoted rate into equivalent monthly and 26-period annual rates. It uses the unrounded half-monthly amount for the accelerated simulation, reduces the balance each period, and makes a smaller final payment. The displayed number of two-week periods is a model result, not a promised discharge date.

A calendar year is not exactly 26 two-week periods. Real payment dates, leap years, lender accrual conventions and changes at renewal can produce differences. Both scenarios hold the starting rate unchanged so you can isolate the payment-pattern effect.

Fit the extra commitment into your budget

Check annual cash, not only whether the fortnightly debit looks smaller. Payroll frequency can help you organize the transfer, but receiving pay fortnightly does not create another month's income relative to the same annual salary.

Use the prepayment tool if you prefer a permitted lump sum instead of a permanently larger annual commitment. Keep an emergency reserve and ask how to change frequency without fees.

Does accelerating change my renewal date?

It does not automatically rewrite the contract term. The balance at renewal can be smaller while the current rate still expires on the agreed date. Future total interest depends on subsequent rates and payments.
