England and Northern Ireland SDLT Calculator: Bands and Surcharges

Estimate individual residential SDLT with standard bands, first-time relief and separate additional-property and non-resident assumptions.

Publisher contact: Abdul RaheemPublisher: REO TechnologiesUpdated: October 25, 2026

SDLT is charged in slices, not at a single top rate on the entire purchase price. It applies in England and Northern Ireland; Scotland's LBTT and Wales's LTT need separate calculations. This tool models a standard individual residential transaction, not every company, trust, lease or mixed-use case.

Try the assumptions

Amounts are GBP. Enter your current figures, then press Calculate estimate. The tool does not establish approval.

Apply the correct price bands

The HMRC residential rates, checked October 25, 2026, use these standard slices: 0% through £125,000; 2% on the next £125,000; 5% through £925,000; 10% through £1.5 million; and 12% above that. The relevant thresholds have applied since April 1, 2025.

A £500,000 standard purchase gives £2,500 on the £125,000–£250,000 slice and £12,500 on the next £250,000: £15,000 in total.

First-time relief has a price condition

In the supported relief scenario, £300,000 is at 0% and the portion up to £500,000 is at 5%. A £500,000 purchase gives £10,000, while £500,001 exceeds the relief ceiling and gives £15,000.05 at standard rates. Selecting first-time status does not establish all relief conditions; the conveyancer must confirm them for every purchaser.

Keep the surcharges separate

Additional-property rules can add five percentage points, while the non-UK-resident rules can add two. They can interact, but SDLT residence is its own test; nationality or a broad immigration label is not enough to decide it.

The additional-property scenario here applies its surcharge from £40,000 and assumes the relevant higher-rate conditions are met. Replacing a main home, a later sale, interests owned elsewhere and special reliefs require case-specific review. First-time relief and additional-property status are separate choices in the widget, not simultaneous switches.

Can I treat the answer as my final tax bill?

No. It excludes linked transactions, companies, trusts, mixed use, lease-rent NPV, special reliefs and transactional nuances. Confirm the law applying at your effective transaction date and ask the conveyancer to calculate the final return.

Where do filing and refund deadlines fit?

Use the completion checklist. The return deadline and a later surcharge-refund claim are different deadlines, and refunds are not automatic.
