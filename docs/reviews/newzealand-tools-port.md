## Corrections and limits

- No discontinued First Home Grant award. Separate confirmed assistance from First Home Loan conditions. No invented lender insurance tariff or automatic approval.
- KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal retains NZD 1,000 and requires three recognized years plus provider confirmation; ineligible Australian transfers must be excluded. Government contribution uses the current 25% match, NZD 260.72 cap and NZD 180,000 income test only for confirmed full-year eligibility. Unknown/part-year situations have no computed award. Standard 3.5% employee/employer settings are editable; ESCT is separate and no future policy/eligibility is promised.
- DTI/LVR are descriptive ratios; RBNZ lending-flow limits are not personal caps. Affordability uses actual take-home income and entered assessment assumptions.
- Income tax uses current ordinary resident bands, plus optional 2026/27 ACC 1.75% up to NZD 156,641 earnings. Tax credits/other deductions are confirmed inputs; no automatic IETC, WFF, student-loan, special-income or tax-code determination.
- Rental interest restores the ordinary eligible-use amount without inventing an immediate refund. Ring-fenced losses carry forward and do not automatically offset salary. Property-sale cash budgets subtract actual settlement and tax budgets; being outside bright-line does not establish that a sale is tax-free. Investment-tax inputs require adviser/provider-confirmed taxable income; no automatic FIF/FDR/CV classification.
- Actual quoted rates/fees drive refinance, low-equity, legal and property-work estimates. Revolving credit stops at a facility breach; consolidation compares payments, interest and remaining balances over one horizon. Car balloon payments discount the final balloon and preserve its final liability. Construction uses actual timed draws, never interest on undrawn credit. Healthy-home budgets do not certify compliance.

Sources beside each tool include IRD, RBNZ, Kāinga Ora, Tenancy Services and Sorted. Dated facts and source URLs are in data/newzealand-tool-reference.json. No live bank offers or market returns are fabricated.
