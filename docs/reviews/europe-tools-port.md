## Corrections and scope

- Add zero-interest and all-cash branches, capped final payments and principal-conserving schedules. A mortgage nominal rate is not fee-inclusive ESIS/APRC. Rate fixes are not the same as full repayment years; actual daily accrual, rate resets and early-payment charges remain contractual.
- Affordability uses actual take-home cash costs plus an explicitly supplied qualifying-income/multiple scenario. No fixed national multiple, universal 35% approval grade, forced 25-year term, arbitrary 7% deposit haircut or “stretch” credit approval. DTI is monthly debt service / income on the user's stated assessment basis.
- Compare two actual EUR quotes over a common horizon, including remaining balances and cash costs; do not reproduce stale national bank-rate rankings. Non-resident budgets use a confirmed LTV scenario and actual available funds, without inferring nationality-based rates, purchase permission, duties or approval.
- Closing costs sum actual itemized tax-inclusive quotes and the user's actual deposit; remove automatic 20% cash deposit and average notary/transfer-tax assumptions. Property taxes use separately confirmed bases and effective rates for acquisition/annual charges, not one country-wide price percentage; do not imply that upfront tax is an annual mortgage payment. Local assessor/notary requirements, cadastral basis, VAT, relief and buyer type must be independently established.
- Currency conversion requires a dated executable destination-per-source quote, with properly denominated source/receiving fees and an extra spread only if not already included. The app's random generated historical FX trend is not ported. ECB reference rates are informational, not executable transfer offers.
- Euribor is a contract-specific tenor fixing, not ECB policy. Permit negative benchmark input, then add the actual margin and apply only an expressly selected contractual ALL-IN floor. The simplified repayment model scopes final nominal rate to 0–100%; index-only floors, negative final contract rates and future reset paths need separate contract-specific work. No static current tenor rate, fabricated historic peak, licensed live series or promised interest saving. The upward shock is a sensitivity assumption.
- Root inputs for price/deposit/rate/cost allowance are blank. Country options are location labels without approximate national tax/DTI assertions. Country tab/dropdown changes clear the result and do not recalculate. Reset synchronizes tabs/dropdown. Successful submission focuses/scrolls to output; all-cash chart rendering is zero-safe.
