## Material corrections

- Replace the app's old new-regime INR 3/7/10/12/15 lakh bands with tax-year 2026–27 ordinary resident bands at INR 4/8/12/16/20/24 lakh. Include the resident rebate through INR 12 lakh, its marginal relief, 4% cess and nearest-INR-10 rounding. Scope is under-60 resident individuals, ordinary slab income up to INR 50 lakh in each separately prepared taxable scenario. No surcharge, special-rate gains, gross-salary deduction preparation, foreign-credit, agriculture or election engine is claimed. Current Income-tax Act 2025 sections 202 and 156 were checked in the official Act as amended by Finance Act 2026. Legacy 80C/24(b) topics require confirmed amounts/remaining caps, not automatic entitlements or marginal-rate-only savings.
- PMAY-U 2.0 ISS numerical screens replace obsolete CLSS category awards. No automatic INR 267,000 grant or artificial 6.5-point rate discount. Scheme approval and staged release are separate; the recast tools subtract only an actual posted credit. Current published income/loan/property/area/tenure conditions and the INR 180,000 release cap are sourced, not treated as an individual award.
- Property withholding uses aggregate consideration/stamp value, inclusive INR 50 lakh threshold and a confirmed ordinary resident-seller/PAN/full-settlement scope. No split threshold among buyers. Current Form 141 guidance is linked; all schedules do not use 1%, and non-resident/higher-rate/certificate/instalment cases are excluded from this limited scenario.
- Quotes replace static bank tariffs, CIBIL-derived pricing, gold valuations, city prices and FX. NRI income uses an entered executable conversion; source and destination transfer fees retain their own units. No rate interpolations or forecast are invented.
- Duty tools require actual state/UT assessed value, effective rate and separately confirmed fees/relief. GST accepts a confirmed effective tax rate and inclusive/exclusive consideration without a second land deduction or automatic affordable-housing classification. Sale cash gains are separate from adviser-confirmed taxable gains and settlement cash.
- Education moratorium interest is simple on a stated full initial draw, paid separately or capitalized once; no universal compounding or subsidy. Construction uses actual stage cash draws and interest only on funded principal, with remaining repayment years after building. Gold principal uses actual eligible valuation and approved LTV.
- PPF interest accrues on eligible monthly balances and credits annually, with explicit fifth-day timing and fiscal month. EPF uses actual net employee/employer EPF credits, excluding EPS/EDLI/admin, rather than a universal 3.67% employer split. SIP/NPS use explicit effective net returns and contribution timing, allow losses, and do not promise future rates, annuity eligibility or tax-free withdrawal. NRE/NRO tax is a supplied maturity-budget assumption, not an automatic residency/treaty conclusion.

All 39 tools and the root use manual Calculate, clear stale results on edit/reset, and require current personal data. Full schedules, CSV, zero-interest and invalid cases are included. Official references are displayed beside each tool and dated facts are in data/india-tool-reference.json. The root has blank loan/rate/actual-fee inputs and focus/scroll after successful submission; misleading AI/property-registry resource labels are corrected.
