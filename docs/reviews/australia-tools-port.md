## Material corrections

- Offset cash remains a separate asset. It reduces the interest-bearing balance while the original P&I payment stays fixed; it does not reduce the loan principal or automatically lower contractual repayments. Optional contributions/withdrawals occur after the monthly payment; interest benefit is capped at outstanding principal. Extra product fees are explicit. Actual daily accrual and cash timing can differ.
- LMI uses an actual all-in quote or an explicitly entered rate scenario, with no insurer tariff invented from LVR, automatic GST plus approximate 9% duty, hard-coded 6.09% payment rate or unconfirmed guarantee waiver. The root requires the all-in quote or an explicit zero exclusion.
- Household affordability uses actual take-home income, living costs and commitments at an entered quote plus editable 3-point scenario buffer. DTI is debt / gross qualifying income; the 6×/20% APRA measure is a lender-flow control, not a personal six-times borrowing entitlement. No automatic income-tax estimate or 30%-of-gross approval test.
- Published general residential duty schedules cover NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT. NSW uses the 2026/27 indexed bands and AUD 3.87m residential premium threshold. ACT's confirmed owner-occupier scenario is separate from its non-owner-occupier base schedule; the root uses the latter. NT includes its lower-value quadratic formula and 3m/5m rate changes, corroborated against the 2026/27 budget. Per-$100-or-part rules are applied where published. State assessor rounding and special transactions require confirmation.
- Concessions, foreign duty, grant funds, FHSS releases and closing costs are confirmed inputs. A federal guarantee is not cash to add to savings. Remove the app’s invented AUD 18,000 LMI saving, universal AUD 900,000 eligibility and automatically counted AUD 50,000 FHSS amount. Settlement timing and provider approval matter.
- Construction starts with actual land/initial principal already drawn, then actual stage amounts at entered start months. Funding cannot exceed the facility or build period. Interest is paid separately in cash; post-build P&I uses funded principal and repayment years remaining after construction. No assumed stage allocation, silent fractional-month rounding or undrawn-facility interest.
- Refinance uses actual quotes, cash fees/penalties/cashback, balances and interest over a shared horizon. A longer amortization reducing the payment is not labeled a guaranteed saving. The existing root offset schedule preserves principal and explicit zero-interest handling.

Primary sources are published beside each tool: ASIC Moneysmart, APRA, Housing Australia/government first-home site, ATO and all eight revenue authorities. Dated facts are in data/australia-tool-reference.json. Ordinary residential general-duty scope only; no automatic first-home/home-use/foreign eligibility, mixed-use, large-land premium apportionment, landholder/aggregation or special transfer determination. Use a conveyancer's assessment for these cases. No current bank offer or live insurer premium is claimed.
