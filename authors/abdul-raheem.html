Abdul Raheem is the founder of REO Technologies and the principal software architect behind MortgagePro Global. With extensive experience in algorithmic finance, deterministic calculation engines, and cross-border lending mechanics, he directs all software development, numerical validation pipelines, and statutory compliance frameworks for the platform's multi-currency financial engines across the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, India, and the Eurozone.

Engineering Oversight & Verification Methodology

Deterministic Financial Engines

Abdul designs and maintains the core JavaScript algorithms powering MortgagePro Global's client-side calculators. Each formula is engineered according to national statutory guidelines — including Canadian semi-annual compounding (Interest Act), Australian daily balance offset interest, and Indian reducing-balance EMIs linked to RBI Repo rates.

Automated Boundary & Rate Testing

Under Abdul's direction, all calculation engines undergo rigorous unit and boundary testing (zero deposit, maximum loan amounts, hyper-inflation rate scenarios, and edge-case amortization dates) to guarantee zero-floating-point discrepancy before deployment.

Jurisdiction Expertise

  • United States: Conventional conforming limits, FHA MIP upfront/annual matrices, VA funding fee schedules, and state-level escrow property tax modeling.
  • Canada: Bank of Canada policy rate tracking, OSFI B-20 stress-testing qualification formulas, and statutory CMHC mortgage default insurance tiered premiums up to the CA$1.5M threshold.
  • United Kingdom: Post-April 2025 Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) progressive tiers, First-Time Buyer relief thresholds, and Bank of England benchmark rate dynamics.

  • New Zealand: Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ) OCR benchmarks, Loan-to-Value (LVR) restrictions, DTI framework enforcement, and Kāinga Ora First Home Loan eligibility.
  • India: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Repo Rate benchmarks, RLLR floating spreads, Old vs New Tax Regime deductions under Section 24(b) and Section 80C, and PMAY statutory guidelines.
  • Europe: European Central Bank (ECB) main refinancing operations, Euribor floating rate adjustments, and French HCSF 35% debt-to-income statutory ceilings.
