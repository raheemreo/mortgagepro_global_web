How this estimate works

Available down payment = confirmed FHSA + HBP + separate savings + co-buyer contribution − closing reserve. The target-price minimum uses the current graduated down-payment rule. The deposit-only ceiling does not test income or affordability.

Assumptions and limits

  • HBP is a repayable RRSP withdrawal with a CAD 60,000 per-person ceiling in this scenario; the tool does not confirm eligibility or repayment start dates.
  • FHSA contribution room is generally CAD 8,000/year and CAD 40,000 lifetime, but investment growth can make a qualifying withdrawal larger. Enter the provider-confirmed available balance rather than a contribution limit.
  • FHSA/HBP participation and qualifying withdrawals require CRA conditions. The discontinued First-Time Home Buyer Incentive is not presented as available. Avoid counting the same savings twice.
