How this estimate works

Available qualifying P&I is the smaller of the GDS and TDS allowances, after entered tax, heat, half condo fees and ground rent. A bounded search includes financed CMHC premiums, current minimum down payment and the qualifying rate when solving for price.

Assumptions and limits

  • The cash down amount must be available after closing costs and any premium tax; those cash reserves are not automatically deducted.
  • The 39/44 defaults are editable planning ceilings. A result within them does not prove lender or CMHC eligibility.
  • Insured amortization above 25 years requires confirmed eligibility; otherwise the modeled price is limited to the uninsured deposit case.
  • Amounts and outputs are CAD. Enter actual contract terms and current quotes; no mortgage rate is inferred from a credit score or Bank of Canada policy rate.
  • A constant-rate amortization beyond the current mortgage term is a planning projection. Rates may change at renewal; lender fees, eligibility, appraisal and contract rounding can differ.
