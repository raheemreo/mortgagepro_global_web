How this estimate works

Ontario, Toronto, BC, Manitoba and Montreal use progressive published brackets. Alberta models one title transfer at CAD 50 + CAD 5 per CAD 5,000 or part. NB uses 1% of the legal tax base. NS uses your municipal rate; other Quebec/Saskatchewan use an entered assessment. Add confirmed surcharges/costs and subtract an eligible base-tax rebate.

Assumptions and limits

  • The tax base can be the greater of consideration and an adjusted assessed/market value; use the applicable legal amount rather than automatically entering sale price.
  • Toronto uses the April 1, 2026 high-value bands. BC includes the additional residential portion above CAD 3 million. Standard rebates, foreign taxes and special transactions require individual review and are not inferred.
  • Alberta mortgage registration and extra titles are additional; enter them in other quoted costs. Outside Montreal, Quebec local rates/exemptions differ. Saskatchewan title-fee concessions/minimums require the actual assessment.
  • For NS, confirm the municipal rate and any provincial non-resident tax separately. A zero entered cost explicitly excludes that cost; it is not a statement of exemption.
