How this estimate works

For the same nominal annual rate, semiannual compounding gives monthly rate (1+j/2)^(1/6)−1; monthly compounding gives j/12. Payment and effective-annual-rate differences are shown.

Assumptions and limits

  • Use the compounding convention specified by the lender. Some Canadian variable-rate products use monthly rather than semiannual compounding.
  • Holding the nominal rate equal isolates compounding; it is not a comparison of two real offers unless both actually quote those terms.
