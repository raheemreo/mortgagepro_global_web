How this estimate works

Both loans start with the same existing principal. Payments and balances are compared after the chosen number of months. Cash-flow saving subtracts cash fees/penalties and adjusts cashback; the interest-cost comparison also accounts for different principal balances.

Assumptions and limits

  • The remaining amortization is different from the mortgage contract term. Enter the actual offer and a comparison horizon within both amortizations.
  • A simple fee / monthly-saving date is not a full economic break-even. No extra borrowing, automatic penalty quote or capitalized fees are modeled.
  • Amounts and outputs are CAD. Enter actual contract terms and current quotes; no mortgage rate is inferred from a credit score or Bank of Canada policy rate.
  • A constant-rate amortization beyond the current mortgage term is a planning projection. Rates may change at renewal; lender fees, eligibility, appraisal and contract rounding can differ.
