How this estimate works

Standard qualifying rate = max(contract rate + 2 percentage points, 5.25%). The financed purchase balance is tested at this rate. Tax, heating, half condominium fees and ground rent are included before calculating GDS/TDS.

Assumptions and limits

  • Standard owner-occupied 1–2 unit purchase with traditional cash down only. Minimum down is 5% of the first CAD 500,000 plus 10% above it below CAD 1.5 million; at CAD 1.5 million or more the model requires 20%.
  • The high-ratio CMHC tiers are 2.8%, 3.1% and 4.0% at base LTV up to 85%, 90% and 95%. Amortization above 25 years adds 0.20 percentage points and requires confirmed first-time-buyer or new-build eligibility, up to 30 years.
  • At least 20% cash down gives zero borrower-paid high-ratio premium in this model, which does not mean all lender insurance is absent. Provincial premium tax is entered separately and paid in cash.
  • This does not decide loan approval or apply every insured/uninsured exception. For qualifying stand-alone uninsured mortgages switched between federally regulated lenders at renewal, OSFI does not prescribe the MQR. Remaining contractual amortization cannot increase. Up to CAD 3,000 may be added for related transaction costs; equity take-out is not permitted. Lenders still assess repayment capacity. Confirm the full scope with the lender.
