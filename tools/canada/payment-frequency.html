How this estimate works

Regular payments amortize the principal at the equivalent rate for 12, 26 or 52 payments per year. Accelerated biweekly/weekly use half/one quarter of the monthly baseline, with earlier payoff and an adjusted final payment.

Assumptions and limits

  • Annual counts are a 26/52-period approximation, not an actual calendar or daily-interest schedule.
  • Accelerated plans commit more cash annually. Actual dates, privileges and lender interest/rounding conventions matter.
  • Amounts and outputs are CAD. Enter actual contract terms and current quotes; no mortgage rate is inferred from a credit score or Bank of Canada policy rate.
  • A constant-rate amortization beyond the current mortgage term is a planning projection. Rates may change at renewal; lender fees, eligibility, appraisal and contract rounding can differ.
