How this estimate works

GDS = (mortgage + annual tax / 12 + heat + half the full condo fee + ground rent) / gross monthly income. TDS includes the other debt payments as well.

Assumptions and limits

  • Use your lender’s qualifying mortgage and debt amounts, including applicable credit-card/line-of-credit treatment.
  • CMHC’s common 39%/44% criteria are not universal limits or an approval promise. Half of condo fees is the modeled qualifying amount; your cash budget still needs the full fee.
