How this estimate works

Apply the published state schedule to the legal dutiable value. NSW uses 2026/27 general/premium residential thresholds. ACT uses owner-occupier rates only when separately confirmed. Add entered surcharges/fees and deduct confirmed base-duty concessions.

Assumptions and limits

  • Amounts are AUD. Enter actual quotes, current balances and costs. No current bank offer or insurer price is invented.
  • Constant-rate nominal monthly model. Daily accrual, actual payment dates, rate resets, offsets, fees and lender rounding can change results. A repayment period is different from a fixed-rate product term.
  • Educational scenarios only; no serviceability, scheme/insurer eligibility, tax assessment or loan approval is established.
  • Ordinary residential purchase only. Use the greater/relevant statutory value, not automatically sale price. No automatic first-home or home-use concession, off-plan relief, foreign-status assessment, exemption, mixed-use, landholder or aggregation determination. NSW premium formula is scoped to a standard wholly residential property of no more than two hectares.
  • WA/SA/TAS/QLD/ACT use per-$100-or-part amounts where published. Final state assessment rounding can differ; this is a budget before final assessor rounding.
