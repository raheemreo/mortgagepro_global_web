How this estimate works

Share value = full price × purchased share percentage. Mortgage = share value − deposit. Rent = unsold share value × quoted annual rent rate / 12. Add actual full service charges for the monthly budget.

Assumptions and limits

  • Amounts are GBP. Enter actual quotes, balances and costs. Planning settings are editable assumptions; no lender rate is fabricated.
  • Monthly nominal-rate amortization; fixed-rate projection across the entered remaining repayment period. Actual daily accrual, payment dates, product fees, rate resets and lender rounding can differ.
  • This is educational scenario arithmetic, not a mortgage offer, agreement in principle, tax assessment or scheme/credit approval.
  • Use the lease and key information document. No automatic £80k income eligibility decision, fixed service charge, assumed rent rate or ordinary SDLT-on-share calculation is used.
