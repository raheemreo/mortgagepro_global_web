How this estimate works

Apply progressive residential bands for the selected jurisdiction. England/NI uses the April 2025 thresholds, £300k/£500k first-buyer relief limits, 5% additional-property surcharge and 2% non-resident surcharge when selected. Scotland uses its own bands, £175k first-buyer nil band and 8% ADS. Wales higher rates are separate bands, not a flat surcharge.

Assumptions and limits

  • Amounts are GBP. Enter actual quotes, balances and costs. Planning settings are editable assumptions; no lender rate is fabricated.
  • Monthly nominal-rate amortization; fixed-rate projection across the entered remaining repayment period. Actual daily accrual, payment dates, product fees, rate resets and lender rounding can differ.
  • This is educational scenario arithmetic, not a mortgage offer, agreement in principle, tax assessment or scheme/credit approval.
  • Individual ordinary residential purchase only. Excludes lease-rent NPV, corporate, trust, linked, mixed-use, multiple-dwelling, replacement-home refunds and transitional transactions. Confirm tax status with the conveyancer. Wales has no separate first-buyer LTT relief.
