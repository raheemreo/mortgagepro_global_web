How this estimate works

Original equity-loan amount = original price × equity percentage. Indicative redemption = current approved valuation × the equity percentage. Current monthly interest = original equity-loan amount × current fee rate / 12.

Assumptions and limits

  • Amounts are GBP. Enter actual quotes, balances and costs. Planning settings are editable assumptions; no lender rate is fabricated.
  • Monthly nominal-rate amortization; fixed-rate projection across the entered remaining repayment period. Actual daily accrual, payment dates, product fees, rate resets and lender rounding can differ.
  • This is educational scenario arithmetic, not a mortgage offer, agreement in principle, tax assessment or scheme/credit approval.
  • England’s scheme is closed to new applications. Existing 2021–23 loans start interest at 1.75% in year six, with later adjustments under their agreement. Older agreements use different indexation. Enter your actual current fee rate; this tool does not project invented inflation or promise a new loan.
