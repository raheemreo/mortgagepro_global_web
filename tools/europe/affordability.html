How this estimate works

Cash P&I budget = max(0, take-home income − living costs − other debt). Divide by P&I per EUR principal at entered rate + buffer. Compare with annual qualifying income × chosen multiple; use the lower scenario and add net cash deposit.

Assumptions and limits

  • Loan/property amounts are EUR. Use actual dated lender/notary/tax quotes for your jurisdiction and transaction; no country-wide average offer or tariff is invented.
  • Monthly nominal-rate scenario or explicitly labeled equivalent-payment scenario. A fixed-rate deal period can be shorter than full repayment years; daily accrual, later resets, insurance and lender rounding can differ.
  • Educational calculation only. Local law, residency, assessed value, lender approval and tax treatment require confirmation. Your financial inputs remain in this tab, without calculator storage or transmission.
  • No universal EU 35% threshold, automatic 4.5× entitlement, hidden seven-percent deposit haircut or forced 25-year term. Budget limits are entered assumptions, not underwriting approval.
