KiwiSaver Home-Purchase Funds Check

Show a conditional first-home withdrawal after the required retention from confirmed eligible funds.

Updated October 25, 2026 · Enter current figures from your documents and quotes. Budget settings are editable. Inputs stay in this tab and are not saved or sent by the calculator.

Rules reviewed 2026-10-25. Use actual quotes and provider-confirmed amounts. KiwiSaver standard rates changed in April 2026; old grant and insurer assumptions are not applied. RBNZ lending-flow controls are not personal approval rules.

Your estimate

Press Calculate estimate after entering your figures.

Rounded display amounts may not add exactly. Calculations use unrounded intermediate values.

How this estimate works

When provider confirmation and at least three recognized membership years are entered, modeled withdrawal = max(0, eligible balance − NZD 1,000). Otherwise no withdrawal is estimated.

Assumptions and limits

  • Amounts are NZD. Enter actual current rates, balances and cost quotes; no bank offer or personal eligibility is invented.
  • Constant-rate monthly nominal model or an explicitly labeled equivalent-period scenario. Actual daily accrual, payment dates, fees, rate fixes and lender rounding can differ.
  • Educational arithmetic only. Provider, lender, IRD, tenancy and legal eligibility need independent confirmation. A repayment period differs from a fixed-rate deal period.
  • Exclude ineligible Australian transfers and other non-withdrawable funds from the entered balance. Recognized membership alone does not establish eligibility. No discontinued grant is added.
