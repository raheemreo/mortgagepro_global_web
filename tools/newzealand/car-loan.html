How this estimate works

Principal = price − deposit + financed charges. Solve equal regular monthly payments after discounting any final balloon. Show outstanding balance after the entered regular payments, before balloon settlement.

Assumptions and limits

  • Amounts are NZD. Enter actual current rates, balances and cost quotes; no bank offer or personal eligibility is invented.
  • Constant-rate monthly nominal model or an explicitly labeled equivalent-period scenario. Actual daily accrual, payment dates, fees, rate fixes and lender rounding can differ.
  • Educational arithmetic only. Provider, lender, IRD, tenancy and legal eligibility need independent confirmation. A repayment period differs from a fixed-rate deal period.
  • Nominal reducing-balance quote, not a flat-rate or true APR comparison. No fake establishment fee, new credit, late fees, rate changes or early-settlement quote is inferred.
