How this estimate works

Round taxable income to the nearest INR 10 after dropping paisa. New slabs: 0/5/10/15/20/25/30% at INR 4/8/12/16/20/24 lakh boundaries. Model the resident rebate through INR 12 lakh and marginal relief just above it. Old under-60 slabs use INR 2.5/5/10 lakh boundaries and rebate up to INR 5 lakh. Add 4% cess and round final tax.

Assumptions and limits

  • All amounts are INR unless a field explicitly says foreign units. Personal prices, balances and rates need your current documents or executable quotes.
  • Constant-rate or explicitly stated scenario arithmetic. Actual daily interest, contractual payment dates, rounding, rate resets and fees can differ. No financial inputs are saved or sent by these calculators.
  • Educational budgets, not approval, a tax return or personalized advice. Confirm legal, lender, payroll, scheme and tax conditions for your own case.
  • Tax year 2026–27, resident individual UNDER 60, only ordinary slab-rate income up to INR 50 lakh. These are taxable totals, not gross salary. Excludes special-rate income, surcharge, agricultural integration, arrears and other credits/relief; no tax-regime election or deduction entitlement is determined.
