How this estimate works

Admitted amount = lower of confirmed qualifying deduction, confirmed remaining limit and income in the old-regime scenario. Compare ordinary tax before/after, including resident rebate and cess. The new-regime option admits zero for this old-regime deduction.

Assumptions and limits

  • All amounts are INR unless a field explicitly says foreign units. Personal prices, balances and rates need your current documents or executable quotes.
  • Constant-rate or explicitly stated scenario arithmetic. Actual daily interest, contractual payment dates, rounding, rate resets and fees can differ. No financial inputs are saved or sent by these calculators.
  • Educational budgets, not approval, a tax return or personalized advice. Confirm legal, lender, payroll, scheme and tax conditions for your own case.
  • Familiar 80C / 24(b) legacy names map to current Income-tax Act 2025 topics (including sections 123 / 22 in scope). No automatic INR 1.5 lakh / 2 lakh entitlement or deduction double-counting. Not a let-out-property computation; ordinary resident under-60 tax model only, without special-rate income or surcharge.
