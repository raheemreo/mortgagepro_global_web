How this estimate works

For the confirmed covered full-settlement scenario, when either aggregate consideration or stamp value reaches INR 50 lakh, model 1% of the higher base; allocate the resulting withholding using your buyer share.

Assumptions and limits

  • All amounts are INR unless a field explicitly says foreign units. Personal prices, balances and rates need your current documents or executable quotes.
  • Constant-rate or explicitly stated scenario arithmetic. Actual daily interest, contractual payment dates, rounding, rate resets and fees can differ. No financial inputs are saved or sent by these calculators.
  • Educational budgets, not approval, a tax return or personalized advice. Confirm legal, lender, payroll, scheme and tax conditions for your own case.
  • Aggregate transaction threshold is not divided by buyers or sellers. Not for instalment timing, non-resident sellers, certificates, higher-rate PAN cases or excluded agricultural land. Form 141 is current in-scope reporting; all its schedules do not share a 1% rate. TDS is part of the seller payment, not an additional purchase tax.
