How this estimate works

Compare annual income up to INR 9 lakh, loan up to INR 25 lakh, property up to INR 35 lakh, carpet area up to 120 sqm and tenure more than five years. Show only numeric screen results; no automatic eligibility or award.

Assumptions and limits

  • All amounts are INR unless a field explicitly says foreign units. Personal prices, balances and rates need your current documents or executable quotes.
  • Constant-rate or explicitly stated scenario arithmetic. Actual daily interest, contractual payment dates, rounding, rate resets and fees can differ. No financial inputs are saved or sent by these calculators.
  • Educational budgets, not approval, a tax return or personalized advice. Confirm legal, lender, payroll, scheme and tax conditions for your own case.
  • PMAY-U 2.0 ISS differs from historic CLSS. Published subsidy terms include 4% on up to INR 8 lakh for up to 12 years and conditional staged release capped at INR 1.80 lakh. Ownership, prior assistance, sanction/disbursement dates and other conditions still apply; use the official scheme calculator/lender.
