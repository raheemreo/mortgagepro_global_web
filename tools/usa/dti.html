How this estimate works

Qualifying income = primary + co-borrower + other income × entered share. Front-end DTI = housing ÷ income; back-end DTI = (housing + other debts) ÷ income.

Assumptions and limits

  • Enter gross income before deductions. Enter recurring debt minimums rather than everyday living expenses.
  • A 28% housing or 36% total ratio is a budgeting convention, not a universal underwriting ceiling. Programs, income documentation and lender rules differ.
