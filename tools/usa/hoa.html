How this estimate works

A 30-year P&I payment is combined with tax, insurance and HOA. The purchase-budget comparison solves the payment and tax formula with and without dues at the entered debt budget.

Assumptions and limits

  • The 43% starting budget is editable and is not a universal underwriting limit.
  • PMI, special assessments and future HOA increases are excluded; include them separately when relevant.
