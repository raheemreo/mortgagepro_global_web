How this estimate works

Origination and points use loan amount; transfer tax uses price. Appraisal, credit report, title, settlement and recording use your quoted dollar amounts. Prepaid interest uses a 365-day year. Seller credit reduces costs but cannot fund the down payment in this budget.

Assumptions and limits

  • Enter the itemized charges from your current Loan Estimate or closing disclosure. No fixed appraisal, title or settlement allowances are inserted.
  • Local taxes, lender fees and permitted seller credits vary. Replace the assumptions with the Loan Estimate; this model does not verify concession limits.
