How this estimate works

Origination, points and lender title use loan amount; owner title and transfer tax use price. Prepaid interest uses a 365-day year. Seller credit reduces costs but cannot fund the down payment in this budget.

Assumptions and limits

  • Fixed illustrative allowances are $600 appraisal, $30 credit report, $1,000 settlement and $200 recording. Owner title is 0.3% of price and lender title is 0.18% of loan.
  • Local taxes, lender fees and permitted seller credits vary. Replace the assumptions with the Loan Estimate; this model does not verify concession limits.
