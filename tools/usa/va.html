How this estimate works

Funding fee is calculated on price less down payment. Purchase fee tiers are 2.15% first use or 3.3% subsequent use below 5% down, 1.5% at 5% down, and 1.25% at 10% down. A confirmed exemption sets it to zero.

Assumptions and limits

  • The purchase fee schedule is shared by Veterans, active-duty service members and National Guard/Reserve members.
  • No monthly PMI is added. Confirm eligibility, exemption, entitlement and any additional cash requirement with VA and your lender. This is not a COE check.
