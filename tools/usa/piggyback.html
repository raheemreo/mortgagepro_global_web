How this estimate works

The first loan is 80% of price over 30 years; the second covers price less the first loan and cash down over 10 years. A single-loan comparison uses the same first-loan rate and an illustrative PMI table.

Assumptions and limits

  • Cash down must be 0–20% for this 80% first-loan structure. Both loans are modeled as fully amortizing fixed-rate loans.
  • The 10-year comparison measures cash payments and ends estimated PMI at the modeled 80% date. Different remaining balances and closing costs must be considered separately.
