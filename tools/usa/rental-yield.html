How this estimate works

Effective income subtracts vacancy from annual rent. NOI subtracts tax, insurance, management and operating expenses before debt service. Cap rate = NOI ÷ price; cash flow subtracts 30-year debt service.

Assumptions and limits

  • Cash-on-cash uses down payment only; include closing costs and reserves when assessing a full investment. It is undefined for zero cash down.
  • No appreciation, selling costs, tax, PMI, future rent changes or major capital expenditures are included. DSCR is undefined when there is no debt service.
