How this estimate works

Mortgage interest is prorated if average qualified debt exceeds the entered debt cap. SALT is limited to the entered applicable cap. Medical expenses count only above 7.5% of AGI. Incremental benefit compares itemizing with the entered standard deduction at the entered marginal rate.

Assumptions and limits

  • You must supply the applicable standard deduction and income-adjusted SALT cap. Zero means an explicit zero assumption; the page does not select a tax year or determine your legal cap.
  • Qualified debt rules, grandfathered loans, filing status, additional deductions, charity limits, AMT and phaseouts are not automatically evaluated.
  • This is a deduction scenario, not a tax return, refund estimate or tax advice. Confirm figures with a qualified tax professional.
