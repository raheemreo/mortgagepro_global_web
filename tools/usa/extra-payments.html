How this estimate works

Each month, interest = opening balance × annual rate ÷ 12. The rest of the scheduled payment plus extra reduces principal. The final payment is capped at the remaining balance and interest.

Assumptions and limits

  • Extra payments start with the first modeled payment and repeat every month. Enter today’s balance as the price and zero down payment to model an existing loan.
  • Savings compare the same starting balance, rate and remaining term with and without extra payments. This model excludes prepayment penalties and fees.
  • Month 1 is the first modeled payment, rather than a fixed historical calendar date. Download the monthly CSV to review the schedule.
