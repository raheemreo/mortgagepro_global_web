How this estimate works

Financing is simple interest on the purchase loan during the hold. Total cost includes purchase, rehab, interest, closing, selling, holding and other costs. Profit is resale value less total cost.

Assumptions and limits

  • Initial cash includes purchase equity, rehab, buying costs and other costs; interest and holding costs may require additional cash during the project.
  • The 70% offer rule is only a rough benchmark. Annualized ROI is simple scaling, not a compounded return. Sale price, timeline and budget overruns can change the outcome.
