How this estimate works

A distance-and-size base allowance rises 20% in peak season. Fuel, insurance allowance, materials, packing, storage, travel and tips are added, followed by a 10% contingency.

Assumptions and limits

  • All allowances are illustrative and are not current mover quotations. Actual weight, distance, access, stairs and dates can materially change cost.
  • Obtain an itemized mover estimate and clarify valuation coverage, deposits and storage terms.
