How this estimate works

Subtotal is the sum of your quoted moving service, fuel, insurance, packing materials/service, storage, travel and tips. Contingency = subtotal × your chosen budget percentage.

Assumptions and limits

  • Use itemized written estimates and avoid counting a charge twice if the main quote already includes it. No distance/room-size price table or seasonal markup is applied.
  • The contingency is your planning choice, not a market price. Actual weight, access and service changes can affect the bill.
