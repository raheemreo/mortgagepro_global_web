How this estimate works

Assistance is limited to the entered down payment plus closing costs. Remaining cash = down payment + closing costs − applied assistance. Repayable assistance uses monthly amortization.

Assumptions and limits

  • Enter an amount confirmed with a state housing agency or program administrator. This is a budget tool, not a grant search or eligibility score.
  • Occupancy, resale, refinancing and forgiveness conditions may make assistance repayable. Confirm the actual program agreement before treating a payment as zero.
