How this estimate works

The illustrative annual rate uses LTV bands above 80%, 85% and 90%, and credit bands below 680, 680–759 and 760+. An amortization loop finds when principal reaches 80% of the original value.

Assumptions and limits

  • The table is an estimate, not an insurer quote. It does not apply to FHA MIP, VA fees or USDA guarantee fees.
  • A scheduled 80% balance can support a cancellation request subject to conditions. Automatic termination generally uses 78% of original value for covered loans when current; contact your servicer.
  • No appreciation, additional payments or loan modifications are assumed in the milestone calculation.
