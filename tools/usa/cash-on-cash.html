How this estimate works

Annual cash flow = gross income × (1 − vacancy) − expenses − debt service. Cash-on-cash return divides it by total cash invested. Holding-period profit adds constant annual cash flow and compounded appreciation.

Assumptions and limits

  • Appreciation is a scenario, not a cash yield or guaranteed return. The equity multiple excludes sale costs, tax and changes in loan principal.
  • Use total invested cash, including down payment, closing costs, repairs and reserves where appropriate.
