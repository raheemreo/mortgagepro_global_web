How this estimate works

The opening loan amortizes over 30 years. Remaining principal is calculated after the fixed period, then re-amortized over the remaining term. The proposed reset rate is constrained by the initial and lifetime increase caps.

Assumptions and limits

  • This is a one-reset planning scenario, not a live ARM rate feed or a simulation of every future reset.
  • The initial cap is modeled symmetrically for increases/decreases with a zero floor. Actual rate floor, index, margin, periodic cap and reset frequency must come from the note.
