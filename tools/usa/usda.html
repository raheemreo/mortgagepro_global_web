How this estimate works

The financed upfront fee increases the loan balance. The first-year annual fee estimate uses the average scheduled beginning-of-month unpaid principal balance, divided into 12 budget installments.

Assumptions and limits

  • Fee inputs use the USDA FY 2027 notice: 1% upfront and 0.35% annual, reviewed October 25, 2026. Check the notice applicable to your commitment.
  • This is the guaranteed purchase model, not USDA direct financing. Address, household income, adjustments and program eligibility are not screened.
