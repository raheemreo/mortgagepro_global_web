How this estimate works

Project budget = land + build + contingency. Construction interest assumes an average drawn balance of 50% of the loan. The permanent loan is a 30-year amortizing loan.

Assumptions and limits

  • Construction draws and timing determine actual interest. The draw sensitivity table shows alternatives rather than a promised schedule.
  • Construction interest is paid separately and is not added to the permanent balance. Fees, permits, inspections, insurance and taxes must be budgeted separately.
