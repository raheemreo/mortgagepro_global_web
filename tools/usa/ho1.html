How this estimate works

Premium = coverage × 0.57% × 0.7 policy factor × age factor × risk factor × deductible factor. The age factor is 1 through age 15, 1.08 through 30, 1.18 through 50, and 1.35 thereafter.

Assumptions and limits

  • This is a simplified pricing illustration. It cannot quote an insurer, bind coverage or determine whether a home is insurable.
  • Rates and discounts are model assumptions; actual policy terms, state, claims, replacement cost and catastrophe exposure can materially change the result. Annual results round to $10, then monthly results round to $1.
