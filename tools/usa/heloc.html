How this estimate works

Available line = max(home value × CLTV budget − existing mortgage, 0). HELOC draws are capped at that line, accrue interest only during the draw period, then amortize. Home equity loans amortize the requested amount.

Assumptions and limits

  • The default 85% CLTV is a planning assumption, not a lender promise. Existing liens and appraisal values affect available equity.
  • The rate stays fixed in this scenario. Actual HELOC rates usually vary; future draws, fees, principal paid during draw and balloon requirements are excluded.
