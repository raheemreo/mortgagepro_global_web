How this estimate works

The seven illustrative score tiers start at 760, 720, 700, 680, 660, 640 and 620, with example rates of 6.48%, 6.72%, 6.96%, 7.14%, 7.38%, 7.74% and 8.22%. The adjustment shifts every tier equally.

Assumptions and limits

  • The example tier table is not a current lender rate sheet, credit model or prediction of the rate you will receive.
  • Rates are only scenario inputs. Loan type, points, LTV, property, lock date and lender pricing affect actual offers. The comparison holds the term at 30 years.
