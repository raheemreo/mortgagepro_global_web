How this estimate works

Adjusted basis = original basis less depreciation. Recognized gain is capped at realized gain and modeled boot; depreciation-related gain is recognized first in this simplified allocation. Replacement basis is cost less deferred gain.

Assumptions and limits

  • Only qualifying business or investment real property can use this treatment. Deadlines, qualified intermediary, related-party, debt and basis rules are not screened.
  • Rates are assumptions, not a determination that a particular capital-gains, recapture or NIIT rate applies. NIIT is applied only to the capital-gain portion in this simplified scenario.
  • Use a qualified tax adviser and intermediary for an actual exchange. Full replacement value alone does not prove full deferral.
