How this estimate works

Cash down = price × selected down %. Savings months = ceiling of the remaining cash goal ÷ monthly contribution. The scenario table uses 30-year P&I and the separate annual PMI rates you enter for 3%, 5% and 10% down.

Assumptions and limits

  • The savings goal covers down payment only. Closing costs, emergency savings and lender reserves are separate.
  • A zero contribution produces no reachable date unless you already have enough cash. Use actual PMI quotes for each down-payment scenario; enter zero explicitly to exclude PMI.
