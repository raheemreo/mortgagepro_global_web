How this estimate works

Current and new loans are amortized over their entered remaining terms. Simple break-even = closing costs ÷ monthly P&I reduction. Net interest savings deduct all closing costs, including financed costs.

Assumptions and limits

  • Payment savings can result from extending the loan even when total cost rises. Compare both payment and interest.
  • The current payment is reconstructed from balance, rate and term. Servicer rounding, nonstandard repayment schedules, cash-out, penalties and escrow are excluded.
