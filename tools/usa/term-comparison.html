How this estimate works

Compute the amortizing payment independently for each term at the entered rate. Total interest = monthly P&I × months − loan.

Assumptions and limits

  • The same rate is used for every term so the effect of term length is clear. Actual lender rates often vary by term.
  • Property tax, insurance, fees, investment opportunity cost and future refinancing are excluded.
