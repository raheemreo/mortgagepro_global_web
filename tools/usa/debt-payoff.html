How this estimate works

Each month, interest accrues, minimums are paid and remaining extra is applied to the first active target. At payoff, any unused payment in that month moves to the next debt.

Assumptions and limits

  • This follows a reducing-payment budget: after a debt is repaid, its regular minimum is removed from the following month’s budget. The entered extra continues. It does not assume permanent minimum-payment rollover.
  • Rates and minimums stay fixed. New charges, fees, promotional expirations and lender-specific daily interest are excluded. A remaining balance after 600 months is reported as an unfinished plan.
