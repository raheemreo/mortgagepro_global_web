How this estimate works

Housing allowance is the smaller of the entered front-end budget and total-debt budget less other debts. Taxes, insurance, HOA and estimated PMI are reserved before solving for the affordable principal.

Assumptions and limits

  • These ratios are personal planning assumptions, not lender limits. Enter a tax and insurance budget appropriate to the home you are considering.
  • A 100% cash purchase is outside this mortgage-budget model. Down payment must be below 100%.
  • Closing costs, reserves, utilities and maintenance are additional. A budget that fits a ratio can still be unaffordable for your household.
