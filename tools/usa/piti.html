How this estimate works

Monthly tax = price × annual tax rate ÷ 12. Annual insurance is divided by 12. PMI uses the original loan balance and entered annual rate when down payment is below 20%.

Assumptions and limits

  • PITI includes only principal, interest, property tax and homeowner insurance. The total housing budget also includes PMI and HOA.
  • Tax is modeled on the home price; an assessed value or local exemption may produce a different tax bill. PMI is an initial estimate and can change.
  • Housing-to-income ratio is a budget measure; it is not a complete DTI or eligibility decision.
