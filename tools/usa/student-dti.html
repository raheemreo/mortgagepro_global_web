How this estimate works

Housing includes 30-year P&I, tax and insurance. DTI adds the entered student payment and other debts. The budget comparison solves for price with and without that student payment at the entered debt ratio.

Assumptions and limits

  • Use the qualifying student payment confirmed by your mortgage lender. Reported, deferred and income-driven payments have program-specific treatment.
  • The model does not calculate income-driven repayment, forgiveness or student-loan program eligibility. PMI and HOA are excluded from this comparison.
