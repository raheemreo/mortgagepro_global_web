How this estimate works

Upfront MIP is 1.75% of the base loan. The annual MIP tier uses base loan amount, original LTV and term. The initial monthly MIP estimate is base loan × annual MIP rate ÷ 12.

Assumptions and limits

  • This models an ordinary FHA forward purchase loan, excluding special and legacy streamline programs. The MIP tier threshold of $726,200 is distinct from county loan limits.
  • The down payment, credit, county loan limit and property must be checked separately. This calculator does not determine eligibility.
  • MIP generally runs 11 years for original LTV at or below 90%, otherwise for the mortgage term; actual servicing charges use scheduled balance.
