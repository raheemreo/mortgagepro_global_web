How this estimate works

Owner wealth is modeled resale value less selling costs and remaining mortgage. The renter starts with the unused down payment and purchase closing costs invested. Annual ownership-minus-rent cash differences are invested or withdrawn at a half-year return assumption.

Assumptions and limits

  • A negative renter investment means additional funds would be needed for that rental scenario. Both gains and losses are possible.
  • Mortgage payments stop at the entered term. Taxes, insurance and maintenance stay fixed; rent and home value change at the entered rates.
  • Taxes, PMI, capital-gains tax, changing maintenance costs and investment volatility are excluded. A first crossover does not mean buying wins in every later year.
