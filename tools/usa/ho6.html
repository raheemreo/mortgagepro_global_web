How this estimate works

Monthly premium budget = quoted annual premium ÷ 12. A percentage wind deductible is multiplied by dwelling coverage; other deductibles use the dollar amount entered.

Assumptions and limits

  • Enter a current insurer quote or renewal premium and the corresponding coverage and deductible. No premium is inferred from a nationwide average, risk tier or property price.
  • The monthly figure is an annual-premium budget divided by 12; actual insurer installment charges may differ. Confirm coverage, exclusions and any separate flood/wind policy with the insurer.
