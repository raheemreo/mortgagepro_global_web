How this estimate works

Taxable value = max(assessed value − exemption, 0). Annual tax is rounded to a dollar. The five-year scenario grows assessed value and holds the exemption and rate constant.

Assumptions and limits

  • State shortcuts identify the location only and never fill a tax rate. Enter the actual local rate from your assessment notice.
  • Assessment ratios, special assessments, caps, local levies and exemption qualification are not applied automatically. Growth is a scenario assumption, not a forecast.
