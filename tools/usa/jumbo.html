How this estimate works

The monthly budget combines amortizing P&I, entered tax, insurance and PMI assumptions. Jumbo comparison uses loan amount rather than home price.

Assumptions and limits

  • The $832,750 default is the 2026 one-unit baseline for most of the US. High-cost areas, special jurisdictions and multi-unit properties have different limits. Enter the limit for your property.
  • Jumbo down payment, reserves, PMI availability and underwriting vary by lender. This result is not an approval or a PMI quote.
