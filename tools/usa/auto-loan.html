How this estimate works

Tax = max(price − rebate, 0) × tax rate. Financed amount subtracts cash down, trade-in and rebate, then adds tax and fees if selected. The loan uses monthly amortization.

Assumptions and limits

  • Trade-in is net equity after paying off any previous vehicle loan. Negative equity is not modeled.
  • Tax treatment of rebates and trade-ins varies by state. Replace the modeled tax with your dealer’s disclosed assumptions before relying on the budget.
