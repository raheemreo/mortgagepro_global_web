About MortgagePro Global

Welcome to MortgagePro Global.

MortgagePro Global is an independent, multi-jurisdiction mortgage calculation and home finance engineering platform developed by REO Technologies. Our software empowers prospective home buyers, refinancing borrowers, and real estate professionals across 7 major economies with accurate, mathematically rigorous, and privacy-conscious financial tools.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate ambiguity in mortgage financing. Buying a home is typically the single largest financial commitment of a lifetime, yet mortgage underwriting rules, compounding conventions, and tax consequences vary drastically across national borders.

By engineering localized, browser-based calculators powered by verified central bank data, we provide transparent, instant financial visibility without paywalls, email collection, or commercial bias.

Computational Methodology & Mathematical Rigor

Unlike generic calculators that apply a one-size-fits-all formula, MortgagePro Global implements jurisdiction-specific mathematical models aligned with each nation's statutory lending standards:

🇺🇸 United States: Standard Actuarial Compounding & Escrow Logic

Computes monthly amortization payments via the standard annuity formula while accurately modeling PITI (Principal, Interest, County Property Taxes, Homeowners Insurance, and FHA MIP / Conventional PMI scales).

🇨🇦 Canada: Statutory Semi-Annual Compounding & OSFI B-20 Stress Testing

Implements Canada's legal semi-annual compounding requirement for fixed-rate mortgages ($r_{eff} = (1 + r_{nominal}/2)^{2/12} - 1$), tiered 5%/10% down payment minimums, and the OSFI qualifying stress test (contract rate + 2.00% or 5.25% floor).

🇬🇧 United Kingdom: SDLT Tiers & Bank of England Tracking

Integrates progressive Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) brackets with First-Time Buyer relief thresholds and Bank of England base rate tracker mechanics.

🇦🇺 Australia: Daily Balance Offset & APRA Serviceability Buffers

Simulates dynamic 100% mortgage offset accounts calculating interest on daily balances ($I = \max(0, P - \text{Offset}) \times \frac{r}{365}$) alongside APRA's 3.00% serviceability stress test buffers.

🇳🇿 New Zealand: RBNZ LVR Speed Limits & KiwiSaver Integration

Models Reserve Bank of New Zealand Loan-to-Value (LVR) restrictions, DTI ratios, and KiwiSaver first-home deposit withdrawals.

🇮🇳 India: Reducing-Balance EMI & Section 24(b) / 80C Tax Optimizers

Computes monthly reducing-balance EMIs linked to RBI Repo Linked Lending Rates (RLLR) and provides detailed breakdowns for Section 24(b) (₹2 Lakh interest exemption) and Section 80C principal deductions.
