Website Data and Deletion Requests

Clear browser preferences or request deletion of website correspondence, with separate guidance for hosting, advertising providers and the Android application.

Document revision 2026-10-25 · Publisher: REO Technologies. Source observations have their own dates.

Different records need different deletion steps. Clearing browser storage can remove a local preference, but it does not remove an email already sent or a service provider's records.

Calculator entries and saved results

The website calculators work in your browser and do not submit your financial inputs to a publisher analytics service. Closing the page removes the active page state. Clear the site's browser data to remove locally stored display preferences where used.

If you downloaded a CSV or copied a result, remove that file or clipboard entry on your device if you no longer need it. Copies shared through another service are governed by that service and the people receiving them.

Correspondence with the publisher

To ask about access, correction or deletion of correspondence, email support@mortgageproglobal.com. Identify the email address or message involved and explain the request. Please do not attach bank records, identity documents or account credentials.

We assess the request under applicable law and may need proportionate information to locate the relevant messages. Retention duties, security needs, mailbox-provider backups and legal requirements can limit immediate removal. We do not promise that every provider copy is erased when a browser cache is cleared.

Hosting and advertising providers

Cloudflare request/security records and Google advertising data are separate from local calculator state. See the provider information and controls linked in our privacy policy. We cannot directly delete data held independently by every third party through a website reset button.

Android application

The mobile application has a separate app privacy policy. Describe the app feature involved when making an app-related request. This website guidance should not be read as a claim that all mobile-app data disappears on uninstalling the app.

Questions about a request

Tell us which page, message or feature concerns you. We do not require a mortgage application to investigate a website issue, and an appropriate request should not include more personal information than is necessary.
