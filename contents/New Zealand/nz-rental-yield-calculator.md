# New Zealand Rental Yield: Include Vacancy and Separate Financing

**Meta description:** Calculate gross and net operating rental yield with occupancy and management costs, without confusing it with after-tax leveraged return.

A gross yield can be useful for a quick comparison, but it does not tell you what is left after operating expenses or finance. Start by saying which costs are included rather than comparing one property's gross figure with another's net figure.

This calculator reports **gross yield and net operating yield before financing and tax**, using the entered property value as the denominator. It is not return on the cash deposit or a capital-growth forecast.

## Use precise costs for the illustration

| NZD 650,000 property, NZD 650 weekly rent | Annual amount |
|---|---|
| Rent at 52 occupied weeks | NZD 33,800 |
| Management at assumed 8% of collected rent | NZD 2,704 |
| Other operating costs entered | NZD 7,000 |
| Net operating income | **NZD 24,096** |
| Gross yield | **5.20%** |
| Net operating yield | **3.71%** |

The 8% is a scenario assumption, not a current market quote. With only 50 occupied weeks, collected rent falls to NZD 32,500, management becomes NZD 2,600 and net operating income becomes NZD 22,900 under the same other-cost assumption.

## Keep costs in the right categories

Other operating costs can include rates, insurance, routine maintenance and body-corporate expenses as appropriate. Do not enter management again in the other-cost field if the percentage field already covers it. Check whether quoted costs include GST and include it in the entered budget where applicable.

Large renovations, acquisition costs, loan interest, principal repayment and tax are not silently included. Net operating yield can be positive while mortgage cash flow is negative. The [IRD rental-income guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/renting-out-residential-property) explains why tax treatment needs its own review; this page does not turn current interest-deduction rules into a universal after-tax return promise.

## Test a realistic year

Allow for vacancy and uncollected rent, and obtain insurance, management and compliance-cost quotes for the actual property. A headline listing yield based on full occupancy is not evidence that every week will be paid.

### Which value should I use?

Use purchase price for a purchase-price yield or a documented current value for a current-value yield, and label the choice. Switching the denominator changes the result without changing cash rent.

### Does higher yield mean the better property?

Not automatically. Building condition, tenancy risk, capital work, location, liquidity and borrowing costs remain outside this ratio. The tool compares entered figures rather than recommending an investment.
