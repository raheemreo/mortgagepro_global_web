# New Zealand Mortgage Calculator: Table-Loan Payments and Refixing

**Meta description:** Estimate a New Zealand table-loan payment using an explicit rate assumption and plan for the remaining balance when a fixed period ends.

A mortgage can take 30 years to repay while its current rate is fixed for a much shorter period. The fix ending does not mean the mortgage ends, and the next rate should be applied to the remaining balance over the remaining repayment term.

For NZD 650,000 with NZD 130,000 deposited, principal is NZD 520,000. At an **assumed 5.99%** over 30 years, the simplified monthly table-loan payment is **NZD 3,114.32**. This rate is an illustration, not a current bank quote or an RBNZ offer.

## Understand what the monthly figure represents

The widget divides the annual percentage by 1200 and models 360 monthly payment periods. Actual New Zealand loans can calculate interest daily, with product-specific dates, fees, offsets and rounding. Use this as a consistent scenario comparison, then check the lender's repayment schedule.

[Sorted's mortgage calculator](https://sorted.org.nz/tools/mortgage-calculator/) is another planning reference. Its [mortgage-types guide](https://sorted.org.nz/guides/home-buying/mortgage-types/) also describes arrangements beyond a standard table loan, including revolving credit and other structures.

## Keep a refixing budget

A short fix makes the rate temporary; it does not make today's full monthly budget certain until the original 30-year end date. Review offers, any break costs, applicable fees and the timing of a change before the current arrangement expires.

After five years in the unchanged example, principal would be approximately **NZD 483,822.82**. If the next rate differs, calculate the new payment using that balance and 25 remaining years. The [repayment comparison](/blog/nz-repayment-calculator) illustrates the different balance after an interest-only period.

## Add the costs outside principal and interest

Council rates, property insurance, body-corporate fees, maintenance and settlement costs are outside the result. If a fee is financed, use the actual principal in the offer. A larger deposit can reduce the debt without necessarily being the best use of every dollar of savings.

### Does splitting fixed terms guarantee savings?

No. Splitting can change repricing exposure, but may affect flexibility, fees and the ability to switch lenders. The calculator does not forecast rates or recommend a particular structure.

### Does this check LVR or DTI eligibility?

No. Use the separate [LVR](/blog/nz-lvr-calculator) and [DTI](/blog/nz-dti-calculator) guides for the policy distinction, then obtain a bank assessment.
