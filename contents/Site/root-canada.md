# Canada Mortgage Planning

**Meta description:** Estimate Canadian monthly payments and a scoped insurance scenario, with separate qualifying-rate and lender eligibility checks.

## Start with the contract balance

Use the property price, cash deposit, rate and remaining amortization. A financed mortgage-insurance premium increases the balance on which interest is charged. Taxes, heating, condo costs and closing cash are outside the principal-and-interest payment.

This fixed-rate illustration converts a nominal semiannual rate to monthly periods. On CAD 520,000 at an assumed 3.99% over 25 years, principal and interest are CAD 2,732.49 monthly. The input is a scenario, not a current bank offer. Variable-rate and daily-interest contracts may use different conventions.

## Insurance and 30-year eligibility

The model uses CMHC's standard traditional-down-payment, owner-occupied one- or two-unit purchase tiers. Insured purchases must be below CAD 1.5 million and meet the applicable minimum deposit. An insured amortization above 25 years requires confirmed first-time-buyer or new-build eligibility and includes a 0.20-percentage-point premium surcharge.

Selecting the eligibility field does not prove it. At 20% down this model assumes no borrower-paid high-ratio premium; it does not say low-ratio lender insurance cannot exist. Any provincial premium tax or closing charge remains a separate cash item. See [CMHC Purchase](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/purchase) and the [premium calculator](/blog/canada-cmhc-insurance-calculator).

## Read the qualifying illustration carefully

The standard uninsured federally regulated assessment uses the higher of contract rate plus two percentage points and 5.25%. [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/minimum-qualifying-rate-uninsured-mortgages) describes its scope and the qualifying straight-switch exception.

The displayed GDS illustration adds assumed CAD 250 monthly tax and CAD 150 heat. It omits other debts, condo fees and a full income assessment, so it is not a pass/fail decision. For an explicit entered-cost comparison, use the [GDS/TDS guide](/blog/canada-gds-tds-calculator).

## Before accepting a renewal

A new rate belongs with the remaining balance and remaining amortization, not the original loan and a reset repayment timeline. Confirm transfer costs, prepayment restrictions and lender assessment. The [renewal planner](/blog/canada-renewal-planner) shows that calculation separately.
