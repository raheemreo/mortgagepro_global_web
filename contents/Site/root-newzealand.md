# New Zealand Home-Loan Planning

**Meta description:** Estimate a New Zealand monthly table-loan payment, a confirmed KiwiSaver contribution and the limits of an LVR illustration.

## The rate fix and the loan term differ

A loan can take 30 years to repay while its current rate is fixed for a much shorter time. At NZD 520,000, an assumed 5.99% and 30 years, the simplified monthly payment is NZD 3,114.32. Daily-interest dates, product terms and fees can change a lender's schedule.

After five years under the unchanged example, the modeled remaining balance is NZD 483,822.82. A new rate should be applied to that balance over 25 remaining years. The [mortgage guide](/blog/nz-mortgage-calculator) explains the distinction between refixing and restarting amortization.

## Enter a provider-confirmed withdrawal

The KiwiSaver field is an amount already confirmed for the purchase, not an unadjusted statement balance. Membership, retention and non-withdrawable transferred funds must be checked with the provider. The tool applies no more than the required deposit to its deposit illustration; an excess is not assumed to be unrestricted cash.

[IRD's first-home guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-individuals/getting-my-kiwisaver-funds-early/getting-my-kiwisaver-for-my-first-home) and the [KiwiSaver article](/blog/nz-kiwisaver-calculator) explain the underlying conditions. A numerical input does not prove eligibility or a release date.

## LVR and DTI are different measures

LVR compares debt with property value. DTI compares assessed debt with qualifying income. [RBNZ's DTI framework](https://www.rbnz.govt.nz/education/explainers/dti) restricts banks' high-DTI lending flows; six times income is not a universal personal hard cap. LVR speed limits also operate within a defined framework rather than granting every borrower a particular deposit entitlement.

Ask the bank to confirm its valuation, serviceability assessment, low-equity charges and any applicable exemption. A result below a threshold does not guarantee a loan. See the [LVR guide](/blog/nz-lvr-calculator).

## Costs outside the estimate

Council rates, insurance, body-corporate charges, maintenance and settlement expenses need a separate budget. A table loan is also not the only structure available: [Sorted](https://sorted.org.nz/guides/home-buying/mortgage-types/) explains other arrangements. Compare the obligations and flexibility before choosing one.
