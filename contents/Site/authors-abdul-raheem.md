## Publication responsibilities

The website provides planning tools and explanations of mortgage costs. Its [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) describes source selection, assumptions, attribution and updates. The [public repository](https://github.com/raheemreo/mortgagepro_global_web) records the published code and changes.

We do not claim that every page has been independently reviewed by a licensed financial professional. Calculator outputs are educational scenarios, and actual lender, insurance and tax decisions require transaction-specific review.
