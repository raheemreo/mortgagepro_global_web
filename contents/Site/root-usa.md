# US Mortgage Budget Planning

**Meta description:** Build a US principal-and-interest estimate and separate tax, homeowners insurance and assumed PMI from actual loan approval and lifetime costs.

## Read the payment components

The base repayment spreads financed principal over monthly periods at the entered annual rate. Property tax, homeowners insurance and assumed monthly PMI are shown separately. HOA, maintenance and closing expenses are outside this root tool's payment.

PITI means principal, interest, property taxes and homeowners insurance. Mortgage insurance is an additional item; it should not be confused with homeowners insurance. Use the [PITI guide](/blog/usa-piti-calculator) to compare the categories with a lender disclosure.

## PMI is an assumption, not an insurer quote

For a supported conventional low-deposit scenario, this tool multiplies principal by your entered annual PMI percentage. A zero PMI assumption is accepted rather than silently replaced with a default. Eligibility, actual pricing and when insurance changes or ends need separate confirmation.

Under applicable cancellation rules, original value, payment history and the scheduled balance matter. An increase in property value does not automatically end PMI. See [CFPB's cancellation explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-can-i-remove-private-mortgage-insurance-pmi-from-my-loan-en-202/) and the [down-payment guide](/blog/usa-down-payment-calculator).

## Lifetime totals are not a forecast

A constant-rate and constant-cost projection holds the entered insurance and tax assumptions unchanged. Actual escrow, taxes, insurance and PMI can change. Do not treat a payment multiplied by 30 years as the lender's guaranteed lifetime ownership bill. Principal-and-interest totals should be compared on the same repayment period.

## Loan programs have separate costs

This basic conventional scenario does not substitute for FHA, VA or USDA eligibility and fee rules. Use the [FHA](/blog/usa-fha-loan-calculator) or [VA](/blog/usa-va-loan-calculator) article tool for its stated program assumptions. A lender must confirm the actual case.

## Before committing

Compare an actual [Loan Estimate](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/), including financed fees, cash to close and items not included in the displayed payment. Credit, documented income, valuation and lender policy are not verified by this calculator. Keep an emergency reserve and obtain transaction-specific advice when needed.
