# Website Privacy Policy

**Meta description:** How MortgagePro Global handles calculator inputs, browser preferences, advertising, hosting requests and messages you choose to send.

MortgagePro Global is published by REO Technologies. This policy concerns the website at mortgageproglobal.com. The Android application has its own [app privacy policy](/app-privacy-policy); website code changes do not by themselves describe every mobile-app feature.

## Calculator inputs and results

Our website calculators process the amounts you enter in your browser. The publisher-controlled calculator code does not submit your loan amount, income, deposit or calculation result to an analytics endpoint. We do not need a mortgage application, credit report, bank account number or identity document to run an estimate.

A result can remain in the page's memory while it is open. If you choose to copy, print or download it, the resulting clipboard entry or file is controlled by you and your device. A browser extension, shared computer or file-sharing service can have its own data practices.

## Optional analytics and session replay

The current publisher-controlled website does not load Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity. Calculation-completion events do not carry financial amounts to an analytics queue. If optional analytics is introduced later, its data fields, consent behavior and disclosures must be reviewed before it is enabled.

This does not mean that browsing the site creates no request data. Hosting and advertising services can process the information described below.

## Hosting and security requests

The website uses Cloudflare delivery infrastructure. A request can reveal an IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, referrer and time of access to the hosting provider. Security features may use cookies or similar technologies to detect abuse and deliver the service.

We do not claim that all hosting data is anonymous, that every IP address is truncated, or that the source code establishes a particular provider-side retention period. Provider processing is described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Request data and security settings must be considered separately from calculator inputs.

Cloudflare also injects a performance-measurement beacon on the live website. The browser sends requests to Cloudflare's measurement endpoint, including page and browser performance information. This is separate from the publisher's calculator arithmetic and from Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity. Cloudflare's [Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/) describes the service. The source repository does not establish every provider-side account setting or retention period, and we do not describe all hosting telemetry as absent.

## Advertising and Google services

The site includes Google AdSense code on content pages. Ad availability depends on Google's account and serving decisions. Google and its partners may use cookies, identifiers, IP addresses and information about visits to this and other sites when providing, measuring or personalizing advertisements, subject to applicable choices and requirements.

Read [how Google uses information from sites using its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Advertisements are not endorsements or mortgage offers from MortgagePro Global.

## Privacy controls

Google's published privacy messages and regional controls, when available for this site and your location, let you make or change advertising choices. The Privacy Settings links use the provider's settings interface when it is available and otherwise lead to this section. You can also use [Google's ad controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and your browser's cookie controls.

Advertising consent signals are initialized to denied before the ad loader runs. That technical default is not a substitute for a published consent message or a declaration of legal certification. The consent message and available ad modes depend on Google's regional and account configuration; declining a choice does not guarantee one particular type of advertisement.

AdSense requests also start paused. If the applicable provider state is unknown or fails, the site does not resume requests. Where EU regulations apply, the additional gate requires a completed provider decision with storage and Google's vendor permission; refusing those permissions keeps requests paused. AdSense evaluates the actual TCF signal for permitted processing. We do not create consent strings or grant consent on your behalf.

The separate [Privacy Settings page](/privacy-settings) loads the existing publisher tag only to make published Google controls available. That page never resumes advertising requests and has no ad units. It remains useful from documents that do not load advertising code. Availability still depends on the actual published provider configuration; this website does not claim to have certified the owner's dashboard setup.

If the provider interface is unavailable, contact us about the issue. You can block cookies or scripts in your browser, although some advertising or security features may then change.

## Browser preferences

The current publisher-controlled calculator code does not save financial inputs or a borrower profile in localStorage or sessionStorage. Browser settings can remove site data and provider cookies where present. We do not use a website login to run the calculators. See the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy) for the distinction between display preferences, security and advertising technologies.

## Contact messages

If you email us, your email address, message, attachments and related correspondence reach the mailbox provider and the publisher. Please share only what is needed for the question. Do not send bank statements, identity documents, application forms or information about another borrower.

We use correspondence to respond, investigate a report and maintain necessary records. We do not promise that all copies disappear immediately: mailbox-provider retention, backups, security and legal requirements can apply. You may ask about access, correction or deletion using the contact below.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a data-protection authority. We assess requests under the rules that apply and may need proportionate information to identify relevant correspondence.

Clearing website storage does not delete an email already sent or records held by an advertising or hosting provider. Our [deletion guidance](/data-deletion-policy) explains those separate steps. Cross-border processing may occur through service providers; their policies describe relevant practices and safeguards.

## Contact and changes

For website privacy questions, email [support@mortgageproglobal.com](mailto:support@mortgageproglobal.com), identifying the page or issue without including sensitive financial documents. The website publisher is REO Technologies.

Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy's updated date. This policy does not claim that a third-party account configuration or every regional consent path has been certified by a source-code review.
