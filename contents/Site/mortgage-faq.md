# Mortgage Planning Questions

**Meta description:** Understand payments, loan terms, mortgage insurance and country-specific rules before treating a mortgage calculation as a lending offer.

## What does a monthly payment include?

A basic repayment formula calculates principal and interest. Taxes, property insurance, mortgage insurance, association charges and maintenance are separate unless a tool adds them explicitly. In US usage, PITI covers principal, interest, property taxes and homeowners insurance; PMI and HOA should be identified separately.

## Is a fixed rate fixed until the loan is paid off?

Not always. In some markets a fixed period can be much shorter than the repayment period. When the rate changes, the new payment should generally be calculated using the outstanding balance and the remaining repayment time. A constant-rate total is a scenario, not a rate forecast.

## Does a lower payment mean a cheaper loan?

A longer repayment period can lower the monthly amount while increasing total interest. Compare fees, financed charges, the remaining balance and a consistent time horizon as well as the initial payment. The [calculator guide](/blog/how-to-use-mortgage-calculator) explains how to set up that comparison.

## Does 20% equity automatically cancel US PMI?

No. Statutory cancellation and automatic-termination rules depend on the applicable original-value schedule and conditions, and a current-value appraisal follows a different lender/investor process. See the [US down-payment guide](/blog/usa-down-payment-calculator) and the linked CFPB guidance rather than assuming appreciation alone cancels a charge.

## Is the Canadian stress-test payment the amount I will pay?

The qualifying payment is an assessment input. Under the applicable uninsured federally regulated framework, the standard rate is the higher of contract rate plus two percentage points and 5.25%, with a scoped straight-switch exception. Taxes, heating, other debts and lender checks still matter. The [Canadian stress-test guide](/blog/canada-stress-test-calculator) explains the distinction.

## Does UK first-time status settle my purchase tax?

No. England and Northern Ireland use SDLT; Scotland and Wales have other taxes. Relief also has purchaser and price conditions. The [SDLT guide](/blog/uk-stamp-duty-calculator) covers the supported individual scenarios, and a conveyancer should confirm the actual return.

## Is Australian transfer duty a single percentage?

No. State, value, date, use, concessions and surcharges can change the assessment. Use the relevant revenue office or the scoped [QLD/VIC general-duty illustration](/blog/australia-stamp-duty-calculator). An LMI or duty assumption is not a verified transaction quote.

## Is New Zealand's six-times-income threshold a personal cap?

The RBNZ DTI framework controls proportions of banks' lending above thresholds. It does not create a universal personal maximum or a right to approval. The [NZ DTI guide](/blog/nz-dti-calculator) separates the ratio from serviceability and available lender headroom.

## Can an Indian or European repayment estimate calculate my tax benefit?

A payment formula does not establish tax eligibility. The tax year, country, property use, ownership and transaction facts need their own assessment. Budget lender fees, local tax and notary costs separately and consult the relevant authority or professional.

## How does credit affect the offer?

Credit history is one factor a lender can consider alongside income, debts, property and product rules. A higher score alone does not guarantee the lowest available rate or approval. Check the lender's actual assessment and the details of competing offers rather than relying on one score.

## Can this website approve my loan?

No. Tools cannot verify credit, documented income, property, insurer eligibility or lender policy. Use the results to prepare questions and confirm actual terms in writing. See the [disclaimer](/disclaimer) and [contact page](/contact) if you need to report a problem.
