# UK Mortgage Planning

**Meta description:** Estimate repayment and supported England/Northern Ireland SDLT scenarios while keeping lender eligibility and purchase-tax relief separate.

## Compare the same repayment timeline

Enter the financed principal, the rate you want to test and the repayment years. A short fixed-rate deal can expire before the mortgage is repaid. Constant-rate lifetime interest is an illustration, not a prediction of every future refix.

At GBP 262,500, an assumed 4.48% and 25 years, the monthly repayment model gives GBP 1,456.08. Actual daily accrual, dates, financed product fees and lender rounding can change the disclosure figures. Keep council tax, insurance, service charges and maintenance outside the P&I number.

## SDLT has a limited scope here

The root tool supports standard individual residential rates in England and Northern Ireland and an assumed eligible first-time-relief category. It does not add additional-property or non-UK-resident surcharges; use the [expanded SDLT article tool](/blog/uk-stamp-duty-calculator) or a conveyancer if those apply. Scotland's LBTT and Wales's LTT need different calculations.

Under [HMRC's current bands](https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates), first-time relief has a GBP 500,000 price ceiling. A qualifying GBP 500,000 purchase gives GBP 10,000; exceeding the ceiling restores the standard calculation. Every purchaser's eligibility and the effective transaction date matter. A checkbox is not proof of relief.

## A repayment estimate does not establish borrowing capacity

A lender assesses income, spending, commitments, credit and the property. There is no universal income multiple guaranteed to every applicant. The [affordability guide](/blog/uk-affordability-calculator) distinguishes budget arithmetic from lender-flow regulation.

## Interest-only and switching decisions

An interest-only payment does not reduce the starting balance. A later repayment phase may have fewer years to clear it. The [repayment comparison](/blog/uk-amortization-calculator) illustrates that difference. For a switch, compare actual fees, early-repayment charges and remaining balances over a consistent horizon; a cashback headline or lower first payment is not the full cost comparison.
