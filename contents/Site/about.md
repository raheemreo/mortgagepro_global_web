# About MortgagePro Global

**Meta description:** Learn who publishes MortgagePro Global, how its mortgage estimates work, and where to find sources, limitations and corrections.

MortgagePro Global is a mortgage-planning website published by REO Technologies. It brings together calculators and educational guides for readers comparing home-loan costs in different countries. You can explore the tools without creating an account or submitting a mortgage application.

## What the site can help you do

A monthly repayment is only one part of a home-buying budget. Our guides explain how principal, interest, deposit, insurance, taxes and fees fit together. Worked examples make the assumptions visible so you can change them for your own planning.

Country pages use different currencies and product assumptions. A Canadian fixed-rate illustration can use a semiannual rate convention, for example, while a simple monthly repayment model elsewhere may divide the annual percentage by twelve. Actual lenders can use daily accrual, different payment dates and product-specific rules.

## Who publishes the information

We do not claim that a licensed mortgage adviser has independently reviewed every page or that a professional designation guarantees the results.

Our public [source repository](https://github.com/raheemreo/mortgagepro_global_web) records the website's code and changes. The [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) explains our approach to sources and examples, and the [methodology](/methodology) explains the models' limits.

## Check the date and the type of rate

A policy rate, a survey observation and a lender's personal offer answer different questions. Rate panels identify their sources and dates. Historical figures and illustrative calculator defaults are not offers available to every borrower. We do not rank or approve lenders for your circumstances.

## Use the results as a starting point

The site is not a bank, mortgage lender, credit broker or financial advisory service. A calculator cannot verify your income, credit history, property, tax relief or scheme eligibility. Confirm an actual transaction with the lender and an appropriately qualified adviser or conveyancer.

If something looks wrong, send the page URL and the relevant assumptions through our [contact page](/contact). Our [corrections policy](/corrections-policy) explains how we assess a reported problem. Please use made-up figures rather than sending private financial documents.
