# US PITI Calculator: Separate the Mortgage Payment from the Housing Budget

**Meta description:** Calculate principal, interest, property tax and homeowners insurance, with PMI and HOA shown separately. Understand escrow changes and omitted costs.

A fixed mortgage rate does not freeze the whole cost of owning a home. The loan's principal-and-interest payment may stay steady while property taxes or insurance premiums rise. That is why it helps to keep the components separate before adding them back into a monthly budget.

PITI means **principal, interest, taxes and insurance**. This calculator shows those four components first, then adds any mortgage insurance and HOA dues in a separate total. It avoids calling every housing charge escrow or PITI.

## Escrow is a payment arrangement, not an extra tax

An escrow account lets a servicer collect money toward bills such as property tax and insurance and pay them when due. Those costs still belong in your budget if you pay them directly. HOA dues commonly go directly to the association rather than through mortgage escrow.

Whether escrow is required or can be waived depends on the loan, lender and applicable rules. Do not assume every VA loan must have it or that every conventional loan with a particular deposit can avoid it. The [CFPB Loan Estimate explainer](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/) tells you to check both the Estimated Total Monthly Payment and taxes, insurance or assessments that are not escrowed.

## Why the payment can change after closing

A tax increase, a higher insurance renewal, an escrow shortage or an adjustment to an escrow cushion may change what you send to the servicer. A fixed note rate does not prevent those changes. Read the annual escrow analysis rather than assuming the increase is new loan interest.

A seller's tax bill can also be a poor estimate for the buyer. Local reassessment rules, exemptions, assessment caps and new construction treatment vary. A sale does not trigger the same tax change everywhere. Ask the assessor what the tax estimate should be for your planned ownership and occupancy.

Insurance needs similar care. Check coverage, deductible, flood or wind exposure, and any separately required policy. The calculator's insurance field should include the annual property-insurance costs you want in that budget; it cannot determine which policies the property needs.

## What is still outside the total?

Maintenance, utilities, repairs, moving costs and a reserve for special assessments are not included unless you budget them separately. Closing costs and initial escrow deposits are also not ongoing monthly PITI. The reserve you fund at closing and the bills it later pays must not be mistaken for two separate annual taxes.

For an FHA loan, use the applicable MIP rather than a conventional PMI guess. For a VA loan, do not add PMI that the program does not require. Use the [FHA](/blog/usa-fha-loan-calculator) and [VA](/blog/usa-va-loan-calculator) guides to identify those loan-specific charges first.

## Questions readers often ask

### Does PITI include PMI?

The four-letter subtotal here does not. Mortgage insurance is shown separately, then included in the broader housing total. Other lenders or tools may use a broader label, so inspect the components before comparing totals.

### Should I enter an HOA amount even if it is not on my mortgage statement?

Yes, if you owe it and want the full housing budget. Paying a bill outside the mortgage statement does not make it optional.

### Does a smaller total always mean a cheaper loan?

No. Compare rate structure, term, fees and the expected balance when you plan to sell or refinance. Tax and insurance assumptions must also match before the totals are comparable.
