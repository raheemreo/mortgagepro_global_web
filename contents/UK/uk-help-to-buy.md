# UK First-Home Support: Help to Buy Status and Lifetime ISA Limits

**Meta description:** Check England's closed Help to Buy equity loan, Wales's March 2027 application deadline and the separate Lifetime ISA conditions.

Old scheme names can stay in search results long after applications close. Before adding government support to a deposit budget, identify the country, scheme, current application status and whether the support is a loan, a guarantee, a discount or your own savings.

## England and Wales have different current positions

[Help to Buy: Equity Loan in England](https://www.gov.uk/help-to-buy-equity-loan) is closed to new applications. Existing borrowers still need to manage their original loan terms, interest and eventual repayment; closure does not cancel those obligations.

The [Welsh Government's current scheme page](https://www.gov.wales/help-buy-wales) says applications for Help to Buy – Wales must be submitted by **March 31, 2027**. It concerns qualifying new-build purchases from participating builders and has its own eligibility, property and affordability requirements.

## A Lifetime ISA is not the same product

[GOV.UK's Lifetime ISA overview](https://www.gov.uk/lifetime-isa) sets a **£4,000 annual contribution limit** and a **25% bonus up to £1,000 per year**, with age and account conditions. It is not a £12,000 total-contribution product with a £3,000 lifetime bonus cap.

For a qualifying first-home withdrawal, [the withdrawal guidance](https://www.gov.uk/lifetime-isa/withdrawing-money-from-your-lifetime-isa) includes a price limit of £450,000 and other conditions, including the timing of the first account payment and conveyancer involvement. That price limit differs from the £500,000 SDLT first-time relief ceiling. A purchase can fit one rule and fail the other. Non-qualifying withdrawals can incur a charge.

## Announced does not mean available

On September 26, 2026 the government [announced an intended Your First Home scheme](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-first-time-buyer-scheme-to-be-confirmed-at-budget), with further Budget detail and implementation timing to follow. As of this October 25 review, this guide does not treat that announcement as an open application route or confirmed money for a purchase.

Shared Ownership, First Homes and mortgage-guarantee arrangements also have different structures and local/product eligibility. They are not interchangeable replacements for an equity loan. Check the relevant official route before booking a property around it.

## Before committing to a purchase

Obtain written confirmation of eligibility, application deadlines, participating property/lender requirements and what happens if completion is delayed. Model ongoing rent, equity-loan interest or fees where applicable, not just the initial deposit reduction.

### Is support automatically the cheaper route?

No. Lower upfront cash can come with other obligations, restrictions and future costs. The right comparison includes the full ownership arrangement and an independent affordability review.
