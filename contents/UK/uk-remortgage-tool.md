# UK Remortgage Costs: Compare Fees, ERC and the Same Remaining Term

**Meta description:** Compare a UK remortgage's monthly cash flow with upfront fees and early-repayment costs while keeping the repayment term consistent.

A lower advertised rate is only part of a remortgage comparison. Ask what it costs to leave the old product, enter the new one and keep repayment on the same remaining timeline. A smaller monthly payment achieved by restarting a long term can be misleading.

The widget compares the same outstanding principal and remaining term at two assumed rates. It subtracts entered upfront costs from the monthly-payment difference over your chosen horizon.

## Obtain the charge instead of guessing its schedule

An ERC may depend on the product, redemption date, amount repaid and allowance already used. A hypothetical 3% charge on £262,500 is **£7,875**, but that percentage must come from your mortgage offer or a current redemption statement.

A five-year fix with exactly two years elapsed has about three years remaining, not two. If you want a 24-month comparison, enter 24 months explicitly rather than presenting it as the remainder of that five-year example.

With an assumed £120 monthly saving and £7,875 upfront charge, simple payment-based break-even is **65.63 months**, before other fees. At 24 months, the modeled cash difference is £2,880 minus £7,875, or **−£4,995**. This does not compare final balances or investment returns.

## Read the break-even label narrowly

Fees financed into a new loan affect principal and interest; enter an appropriately adjusted loan comparison with the lender rather than assuming an upfront-cash model covers them. Different future rates, outstanding balances, tax and product incentives are outside the simple result.

[MoneyHelper's remortgaging guidance](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/remortgaging-to-cut-costs) explains why costs and timing matter. A same-lender product transfer can have different fees and checks; it is not automatically the cheapest or always free of underwriting.

## Plan the completion date

A rate reservation is not the same as redeeming the current mortgage. Check the exact ERC end date, offer validity, legal requirements and when the new loan starts. Do not assume the charge always ends on the same date as the headline fixed-rate period.

### Can an overpayment reduce the later charge?

It can change the balance used, but allowance and redemption clauses can interact. Get a quote for the actual sequence of payments and dates.

### What if the new payment is higher?

The calculator reports no break-even from monthly savings when there is no positive saving. A transaction may have other purposes, but this arithmetic does not establish that those purposes justify its cost.
