# UK Buy-to-Let: Model Rental Coverage Without Assuming Approval

**Meta description:** Calculate required rent and an interest-coverage loan scenario using editable ICR and stress-rate inputs, without treating company status as automatic eligibility.

Rent is important to a buy-to-let assessment, but it is not the only thing a lender checks. Personal income, credit, portfolio commitments, property condition and the proposed ownership structure can all matter. A rent calculation is not a substitute for that review.

This tool uses an entered stressed interest rate and interest coverage ratio, or ICR. It calculates the rent needed to cover modeled interest by that margin and the loan supported by a stated rent under the same assumptions.

## Understand the numerator and denominator

**ICR = gross rent ÷ stressed interest × 100.** With a £300,000 loan and an assumed 5.5% stress rate, monthly stressed interest is £1,375. At a 125% assumed ICR, required rent is **£1,718.75**. At 145%, it is **£1,993.75**.

At £1,800 rent the mathematical ICR is 130.91%. That meets one assumed test and not the other; it does not establish approval or prove that changing to a company would solve the application.

The [Bank of England's buy-to-let analysis](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2023/2023/the-buy-to-let-sector-and-financial-stability) describes how lenders have used stressed coverage. Its historical examples are not a universal 2026 product quote. Obtain the actual ICR, stress rate and treatment of rental income for the lender and product you are considering.

## Do not choose an ownership structure from this ratio alone

Company borrowing can involve different fees, rates, guarantees, accounting duties and taxes. The same headline ICR can also be combined with different LTV or minimum-income requirements. A calculator cannot decide whether incorporation is beneficial for your circumstances.

[MoneyHelper's buy-to-let guide](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/blog/buy-or-rent-a-home/buy-to-let-mortgages-explained) provides further background. Confirm whether the product is interest-only, what principal remains due and how it is to be repaid.

## Test the property budget separately

The ratio uses gross rent. It does not subtract management, void periods, repairs, insurance, service charges, compliance work or tax. A property can meet an assumed rental-coverage test and still have poor cash flow once those costs are included.

### Can salary make up a rental shortfall?

Some lenders consider additional income or other approaches, but the rules vary. Do not assume every lender offers top-slicing or that salary is irrelevant.

### Is a five-year fix always assessed more generously?

No universal result is modeled. Ask for the actual stress method and compare product fees, early-repayment terms and loan balance alongside the initial rate.
