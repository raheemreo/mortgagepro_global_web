# Australia Mortgage Calculator: Repayments and Rate Changes

**Meta description:** Estimate Australian principal-and-interest repayments with explicit rate assumptions and distinguish a monthly model from daily lender interest.

An Australian home-loan repayment depends on the lender's contract rate, not the Reserve Bank cash rate by itself. A variable rate can change, and an initial fixed period can end before the loan is paid off. Treat one repayment estimate as a scenario rather than a lifetime promise.

For AUD 650,000 with AUD 130,000 deposited, principal is AUD 520,000. At an **assumed 6.19%** over 25 years, a monthly principal-and-interest model gives **AUD 3,411.02**. This rate is illustrative, not an available lender offer.

## Use the contract's repayment convention

The widget uses a monthly rate of annual percentage / 1200 with 300 equal payment periods. Many Australian loans accrue interest daily; actual debit dates, month lengths, offsets, fees and product rules can change statement amounts. The monthly formula is useful for comparison, but calling it the exact daily-interest system would be wrong.

Use [ASIC Moneysmart's mortgage calculator](https://moneysmart.gov.au/home-loans/mortgage-calculator) as another planning reference and compare the result with the lender's written repayment schedule.

## Test a rate change without calling it a prediction

Keep principal and remaining term the same and change the rate field to see sensitivity. A lender need not change its retail rate by exactly the change in the RBA cash rate. A quarter-point sensitivity is a hypothetical scenario, not a forecast of the next debit.

If a fixed period has already elapsed, start a repricing comparison from the remaining balance and remaining term. Reusing the original loan for both sides can overstate the payment needed later.

## Build the ownership budget separately

The displayed P&I excludes council rates, insurance, strata costs, maintenance and establishment charges. A capitalized LMI premium increases principal; a deposit percentage does not by itself give the final financed balance.

The [LMI guide](/blog/australia-lmi-calculator), [offset guide](/blog/australia-offset-account-calculator) and [affordability sensitivity](/blog/australia-affordability-calculator) cover different parts of that decision. None is a lender approval.

### Is fixing always safer or cheaper?

A fix provides rate certainty for its agreed period but can restrict extra repayments and carry break costs. Variable products have different flexibility and repricing risk. Compare the contract features and expected holding period rather than assuming a universal best option.
