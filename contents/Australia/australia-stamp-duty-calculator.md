# Australia Transfer Duty: Choose the State and the Right Buyer Category

**Meta description:** Compare supported Queensland and Victoria general transfer-duty examples and find the right state assessment instead of using a national stamp-duty guess.

There is no national Australian transfer-duty schedule. The jurisdiction, dutiable value, contract date, buyer and property use can all change the assessment. Comparing a Queensland home-concession estimate with a Victorian general-duty estimate can create a false picture of the difference.

This widget deliberately supports only **Queensland and Victoria general non-concession rates**, checked October 25, 2026. For concessions or another jurisdiction, use the relevant revenue office's assessment.

## Compare like with like at AUD 700,000

| General non-concession example | Calculation | Modeled duty |
|---|---|---|
| Queensland | AUD 17,325 + 4.5% of AUD 160,000 | **AUD 24,525** |
| Victoria | AUD 2,870 + 6% of AUD 570,000 | **AUD 37,070** |

[Queensland's published rates](https://qro.qld.gov.au/duties/transfer-duty/calculate/rates/) use each AUD 100 or part thereof for applicable bands; the widget includes that rounding. [Victoria's general rates](https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/about-us/rates-and-statistics/current-rates/land-transfer-duty-non-principal-place-residence-current-rates) have a different schedule. The example is not the final duty for a buyer who qualifies for a concession.

## Use the correct jurisdiction's current assessment

For a NSW purchase, use [Revenue NSW transfer-duty guidance](https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au/taxes-duties-levies-royalties/transfer-duty), including the applicable dated rates and concessions. The former [First Home Buyer Choice](https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au/grants-schemes/previous-schemes/first-home-buyer-choice) annual-property-tax option is closed to new entrants; it should not be presented as an available general alternative to duty for new buyers.

For purchases elsewhere, use the relevant state or territory revenue office and its transaction-date calculator. Do not apply the QLD or VIC selector to South Australia, Western Australia, Tasmania, the ACT or the Northern Territory. This guide does not supply an unverified tax estimate for those jurisdictions.

## Check relief and surcharges independently

Home-occupier concessions, first-home relief, new-home or land treatment, foreign-purchaser surcharges and eligibility definitions vary. Citizenship, residence and ownership history can have jurisdiction-specific meanings. A broad statement that every state offers the same relief or that all foreign buyers pay one additional rate is unreliable.

### Can duty be financed?

Ask the lender how much of the purchase costs can be funded within its LVR and affordability rules. Even if financing is possible, duty remains a cost and can increase debt or LMI exposure.

### Is the purchase price always the dutiable value?

Not necessarily. Market value, related-party transactions and other statutory rules can matter. Confirm the value and contract date with the conveyancer and revenue office before relying on the estimate.
