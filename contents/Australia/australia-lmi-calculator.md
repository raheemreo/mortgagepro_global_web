# Australia LMI: Estimate a Quoted Premium and Check Guarantee Eligibility

**Meta description:** Separate Australian LMI scenario arithmetic from insurer pricing and check the current 5% Deposit Scheme without assuming price caps disappeared.

Lenders mortgage insurance, or LMI, protects the lender against a covered loss. It is not income protection for the borrower and does not erase the borrower's obligations. The amount and whether it is charged depend on the loan, lender and insurer.

This estimator uses a premium percentage **you enter**. It does not invent insurer rates from an LVR table. Replace the illustrative 2% default with a lender quote before using the result in a purchase budget.

## Follow the premium into the principal

At an AUD 700,000 price and AUD 70,000 deposit, base principal is AUD 630,000 and LVR is 90%. An assumed 2% premium is AUD 12,600. If entirely financed, principal becomes AUD 642,600 before any other fees or taxes.

That example is arithmetic, not evidence that a 90% LVR premium costs 2% in the current market. Ask for the amount, taxes, financing treatment, resulting LVR and any lender limits in writing.

## The expanded guarantee still has conditions

The [Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme](https://firsthomebuyers.gov.au/australian-government-5-percent-deposit-scheme), formerly the Home Guarantee Scheme, expanded from October 1, 2025. The changes removed income caps and waitlists and permit eligible purchases without LMI through participating lenders. **Location-based property price caps and other eligibility conditions still matter**; they were not all abolished.

A government guarantee is not cash paid into your deposit and does not guarantee loan approval. Check the official [property-price caps](https://firsthomebuyers.gov.au/australian-government-5-percent-deposit-scheme/property-price-caps) and eligibility tools, then obtain confirmation from the participating lender. Do not select a zero premium merely because a deposit reaches 5%.

## Compare paying, financing and avoiding the charge

A financed premium attracts loan interest. Paying it upfront preserves a smaller loan but uses cash that may be needed for other costs. A waiver or guarantor arrangement can have separate conditions and risks. No one choice is automatically best for every buyer.

[Moneysmart's switching guide](https://moneysmart.gov.au/home-loans/switching-home-loans) notes that LMI can affect refinancing and that you can ask about a possible partial refund from an existing loan. Do not assume universal refundability or universal non-refundability.

### Is LMI tax deductible?

This estimate assumes no tax benefit. Borrowing purpose, applicable tax law and the charge's treatment need tax advice. Ask a tax adviser about the treatment for your borrowing purpose before adding any assumed benefit to the comparison.

### Does a higher deposit automatically remove every charge?

No. Ask the lender which costs are still payable even when LMI is not charged. The widget deliberately keeps insurer eligibility separate from the premium multiplication.
