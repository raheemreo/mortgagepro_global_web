# Canada GDS and TDS: Include Heat, Condo Fees and the Right Payment

**Meta description:** Calculate Canadian housing and total-debt ratios with heat, condo fees and ground rent, using an entered qualifying mortgage payment.

A mortgage statement does not contain every housing expense used in a debt-service ratio. Property tax, heating, a portion of condominium fees and applicable ground rent can matter even when paid to someone else.

This tool takes a **monthly qualifying mortgage payment** as an input. If a stress test applies, enter the assessed payment rather than the lower contractual debit. If a premium is financed, the assessed principal needs to include it.

## Use a consistent monthly unit

Under the [CMHC calculation method](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/calculating-gds-tds), the housing side includes principal and interest, tax and heat. Applicable condo fees contribute 50%; applicable site or ground rent contributes 100%.

For an illustrative CAD 520,000 loan at a 5.99% qualifying rate over 25 years with semiannual compounding, P&I is **CAD 3,323.90**. Add CAD 220 tax, CAD 120 heat and half a CAD 300 condo fee: housing costs are **CAD 3,813.90**. With CAD 12,500 monthly qualifying income, GDS is **30.51%**. Add CAD 630 other monthly debt payments and TDS is **35.55%**.

CMHC's reference maximums are 39% GDS and 44% TDS for its applicable framework. These are not a universal OSFI rule that determines the outcome of every uninsured or provincially regulated loan. The calculator therefore reports arithmetic rather than an approval badge.

## Ask how each obligation is treated

A lender determines qualifying income, student-loan payments, credit commitments, joint-borrower liabilities and any permitted exclusions. Enter payment obligations here, not the sum of outstanding debt balances. Conversely, use debt balances in a loan-to-income calculation rather than mixing the two concepts.

Your actual budget should include the full condo fee, utilities, maintenance and savings even when the underwriting ratio counts something differently. Half a fee being counted for GDS does not mean you owe only half.

### Why is the entered mortgage payment higher than my quote?

The [stress-test tool](/blog/canada-stress-test-calculator) compares a contract-rate scenario with the standard qualifying-rate formula. The qualifying payment is an assessment input, not the amount the lender automatically collects each month.

### What if income is zero?

The ratio is undefined. Blank, zero-income or invalid entries clear the output. A previous result should never remain visible as if it applied to the changed inputs.
