# Canada Prepayments: Model a Lump Sum Without Guessing the Penalty

**Meta description:** Calculate the effect of a permitted mortgage lump sum while keeping contractual prepayment limits, renewal rates and penalties separate.

Extra principal reduces future interest when the loan terms permit it, but an attractive interest-saving estimate is not permission to send a large payment. A closed mortgage can limit the size, timing or frequency of penalty-free prepayments.

Use this tool after checking the allowance in the contract. It models a lump sum paid alongside a selected monthly payment and compares it with the same loan without the lump sum.

## Establish the allowance first

The [FCAC prepayment guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/mortgages/pay-mortgage-faster.html) explains that lump-sum and regular-payment privileges vary. Ask which principal amount the allowance uses, when it resets, whether unused allowance carries over, and whether payment increases have a separate limit.

For example, **if** a contract permits 15% of CAD 520,000 per allowance year, the nominal allowance is CAD 78,000. A proposed CAD 30,000 payment does not necessarily fit if earlier prepayments have already used most of that allowance. Calendar-year and anniversary-year arrangements should not be treated as interchangeable.

## What the result includes

The baseline is a monthly semiannual-compounding fixed-rate model. The lump sum reduces the balance after that month's interest and alongside the scheduled payment. The simulation retains the scheduled monthly payment and adjusts the final payment when the loan is cleared.

The savings apply across the modeled amortization with the entered rate held constant. They are not savings guaranteed during only the remaining contract term. Future renewals, rate changes, fees and the value of cash kept elsewhere can alter the outcome.

## Get a written charge before breaking a mortgage

An interest-rate differential, three-month-interest charge, discharge fee or cashback condition cannot be inferred reliably from principal and note rate alone. A general calculator should not invent your lender's comparison rate or decide which balance an allowance applies to.

Ask for an itemized payout or prepayment statement and its validity date. If the decision involves refinancing rather than simply paying permitted extra principal, also compare closing costs and the new repayment timeline.

### Does the model tell me the best use of my cash?

No. It does not compare emergency reserves, higher-cost debt, investment risk or tax effects. It shows one loan-path difference under stated assumptions.

### Can I use it for recurring extra payments?

Use the [FCAC calculator](https://itools-ioutils.fcac-acfc.gc.ca/MC-CH/MortgageCalculator.aspx) or your Canadian lender's tool with the appropriate rate convention for the precise product. This page intentionally models a single Canadian lump sum.
