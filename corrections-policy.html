Corrections Policy

Report a calculation error, outdated financial rule or broken link and understand how MortgagePro Global reviews and corrects published information.

Document revision 2026-10-25 · Publisher: REO Technologies. Source observations have their own dates.

A small error can matter when someone is planning a large purchase. If you find a problem, the most useful report shows the page, the assumptions and the result you expected. You do not need to send personal financial documents.

Report a problem

Email support@mortgageproglobal.com with the page URL and a short description. For a calculation, include made-up inputs that reproduce it, the displayed result and any relevant official source. For a broken link, include the link's destination and what happened.

Please leave out account numbers, identity documents, credit reports, application forms and information about another person. We cannot provide personal mortgage approval or tax advice through a correction request.

How we review a report

We try to reproduce the issue, check the relevant source and distinguish a software fault from a different product convention or an unsupported transaction. A lender's daily-interest schedule, for example, may differ from a simplified monthly illustration without either using the same model.

Issues affecting financial figures or eligibility explanations receive priority. Where appropriate, we correct the source and generated page together, add a regression check and update related summaries. We may narrow or temporarily remove an unreliable estimate rather than leave a misleading answer in place.

Dates and records

A substantial correction receives an updated date. Original publication dates remain separate. Public repository history records code and content changes; it does not expose private support correspondence.

We do not promise a particular acknowledgment time, a fixed repair deadline or a staffed professional review team. The time needed depends on the evidence, the model and whether an external source is available. An automated test passing does not by itself establish that a particular loan can be approved.

Disagreement with a result

Please explain which assumption differs: rate convention, payment dates, fees, insurance, valuation or repayment term. The methodology and each tool's limitations can help locate the difference. Confirm an actual transaction with the lender or relevant professional before relying on a corrected estimate.
