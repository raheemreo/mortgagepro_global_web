Calculation accuracy and limitations

Results depend on your inputs, the calculation model and any assumptions or reference information used. Actual costs can differ due to lender-specific compounding, rounding, repayment timing, rate changes, fees, taxes, insurance, underwriting or changes in law.

Treat defaults and examples as illustrative unless explicitly identified as dated, sourced observations. A central-bank policy rate, a survey rate and a lender quote describe different things. Review the inputs and assumptions and confirm material figures independently before making a commitment.

Regional calculations

  • United States. PITI, mortgage insurance, affordability and similar figures are planning estimates. References to FHA, VA, USDA or conventional lending do not establish eligibility or government approval.
  • Canada. Estimates involving mortgage default insurance or qualifying rates depend on the assumptions used. Confirm insurance and mortgage qualification with the relevant provider.
  • Australia. Estimates for lenders mortgage insurance or stamp duty may differ from actual costs and can depend on the lender, state or territory.
  • India. Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) scenarios depend on the loan terms and inputs. Reference rates do not establish what a bank will offer. Check any tax treatment or scheme eligibility independently.
  • Europe. Lending rules, taxes and property costs vary by country. Check the assumptions for the market you select and confirm any local requirements independently.
  • New Zealand. Check loan-to-value assumptions and any applicable lender requirements. References to KiwiSaver or assistance programmes, where shown, do not establish eligibility.
  • Global. Check the currency, repayment method and assumptions shown for your scenario. A general model may omit local lending conventions, fees, taxes or eligibility rules.
