Official Data Sources & Regulatory Directory

Last verified: August 2026. A transparent compilation of sovereign central banks, government housing authorities, and statutory tax agencies referenced across MortgagePro Global.

Governance Standard: Primary Statutory & Central Bank Sourcing

To guarantee complete empirical accuracy on all financial models, MortgagePro Global adheres to a strict primary-source policy. We reference official sovereign regulators, central bank policy releases, government housing agencies, and statutory revenue departments rather than unverified third-party rate aggregators.

Below is our comprehensive, transparent directory of official data sources organized by jurisdiction.

🇺🇸 United States Official Data Sources

Freddie Mac (PMMS)

Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS) tracking national 30-year fixed, 15-year fixed, and ARM consumer mortgage averages.

Visit Freddie Mac PMMS ↗

Federal Reserve System

Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) target federal funds rate decisions and macroeconomic monetary policy data.

Visit Federal Reserve ↗

CFPB (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau)

Federal mortgage disclosures, Know Before You Owe (TRID) rules, and consumer lending compliance regulations.

Visit CFPB Owning a Home ↗

HUD (Department of Housing and Urban Development)

FHA loan limits, upfront mortgage insurance premium (UFMIP) schedules, and annual MIP guidelines.

Visit HUD ↗

FHFA (Federal Housing Finance Agency)

National and high-cost county conforming loan limits for Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac conventional loans.

Visit FHFA ↗

IRS (Internal Revenue Service)

Publication 936 (Home Mortgage Interest Deduction) rules and property tax deduction limits under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act.

Visit IRS Pub 936 ↗

🇨🇦 Canada Official Data Sources

Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada)

Official policy interest rate decisions and Canadian macroeconomic financial system reviews.

Visit Bank of Canada ↗

CMHC (Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation)

High-ratio mortgage loan insurance premium tiers, down payment regulations (5%/10% tiered scales), and housing market data.

Visit CMHC ↗

OSFI (Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions)

Guideline B-20 Residential Mortgage Underwriting Practices and Minimum Qualifying Rate (MQR) stress test standards.

Visit OSFI ↗

FCAC (Financial Consumer Agency of Canada)

Consumer protections, rights for mortgage renewal, and statutory pre-payment charge calculation frameworks.

Visit FCAC ↗

🇬🇧 United Kingdom Official Data Sources

Bank of England (BoE)

Official Bank Rate set by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) and sterling benchmark financial statistics.

Visit Bank of England ↗

HMRC (HM Revenue & Customs)

Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) rate thresholds, First-Time Buyers' Relief rules, and additional property surcharge rates.

Visit GOV.UK SDLT ↗

FCA (Financial Conduct Authority)

Mortgage Conduct of Business (MCOB) regulatory standards, affordability stress testing, and consumer credit requirements.

Visit FCA ↗

🇦🇺 Australia Official Data Sources

Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA)

Official Cash Rate target decisions, monetary policy statements, and interest rate benchmark series.

Visit RBA ↗

APRA (Australian Prudential Regulation Authority)

Prudential Standard APS 220 credit risk standards, including the 3.00% mortgage serviceability buffer framework.

Visit APRA ↗

ASIC / Moneysmart

Regulatory guidance on National Consumer Credit Protection (NCCP), responsible lending, and key facts sheets.

Visit ASIC Moneysmart ↗

ATO (Australian Taxation Office)

Property investment negative gearing rules, capital gains tax (CGT) discounts, and First Home Super Saver (FHSS) scheme guidelines.

Visit ATO ↗

🇳🇿 New Zealand Official Data Sources

Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ)

Official Cash Rate (OCR), Loan-to-Value Ratio (LVR) restrictions, and Debt-to-Income (DTI) macroprudential standards.

Visit RBNZ ↗

Inland Revenue Department (Te Tari Taake - IRD)

Bright-line property tax rules, residential interest deductibility phase-in schedules, and KiwiSaver tax settings.

Visit IRD ↗

Kāinga Ora - Homes and Communities

First Home Loan criteria (5% deposit underwritten framework) and KiwiSaver First Home Withdrawal statutory parameters.

Visit Kāinga Ora ↗

🇮🇳 India Official Data Sources

Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) repo rate decisions and Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR) benchmark regulations.

Visit RBI ↗

Income Tax Department (Government of India)

Income Tax Act deductions for home loan interest under Section 24(b), principal under Section 80C, and first-time buyer Section 80EEA.

Visit Income Tax India ↗

National Housing Bank (NHB)

Housing finance sector oversight, Loan-to-Value (LTV) ceiling regulations, and affordable housing guidance.

Visit NHB ↗

🇪🇺 Europe Official Data Sources

European Central Bank (ECB)

Key ECB interest rates: Main Refinancing Operations (MRO), Deposit Facility Rate (DFR), and Marginal Lending Facility (MLF).

Visit ECB ↗

Eurostat

Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices (HICP), House Price Index (HPI), and Euro area macroeconomic indicators.

Visit Eurostat ↗

European Banking Authority (EBA)

Mortgage Credit Directive (MCD) harmonisation guidelines and European consumer lending standards.

Visit EBA ↗

How We Keep Sources Synchronized

Our quantitative engineering team continually audits the datasets referenced above. When statutory policy rates or tax laws change, our centralized parameters in js/rates.js and calculation engines are updated in accordance with our Editorial Policy and Corrections Policy.
