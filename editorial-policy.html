Editorial Policy

How MortgagePro Global uses official sources, explains financial assumptions, checks calculations and handles updates without promising independent licensed review.

Updated October 25, 2026 · Publisher: REO Technologies

A financial explanation should tell you what the numbers mean and what they leave out. We aim to help readers prepare better questions for a lender, rather than treating a calculator result as permission to borrow.

Sources and dates

We prefer regulators, tax authorities, government scheme pages and central banks for rules and reference rates. Links beside relevant claims let readers check the original guidance. A lender's quote is still needed for individual pricing and eligibility.

We distinguish the effective date of a rule or rate from the date we checked it. A future announcement is identified as future-dated until it takes effect. An older effective date does not automatically make an unchanged policy rate wrong, but it does not replace a current source check.

The data sources page explains the difference between policy references, published observations and hypothetical calculator inputs. Historical figures are not presented as current personal offers.

Calculations and examples

Worked examples state the principal, rate convention, payment frequency and repayment period. Estimates identify excluded costs and product limits. We use automated numerical and boundary checks for the models covered by our test suite; this does not certify every possible lender product or tax transaction.

The methodology describes payment, balance, insurance and tax assumptions. A calculation that matches a formula can still be unsuitable for a transaction if the wrong eligibility category or input is selected.

Writing and attribution

We write for people using the tools: a clear explanation, a useful example and a practical next step matter more than a target word count. We do not add invented borrower stories, quotations, professional experience or credentials to make a page sound authoritative.

Software and AI-assisted drafting may support research, formatting or editing. The publisher remains responsible for the published material. We do not label AI assistance as evidence of accuracy, promise a detector score or claim an independent human or licensed review that has not happened.

Updates and corrections

Meaningful content changes receive an updated date. We preserve an article's original publication date and keep its summary and structured data consistent with the visible explanation. Automated observation feeds and manually checked policy references have different update paths; neither is a promise of real-time market coverage.

Reports of numerical mistakes, outdated rules and broken links are assessed under our corrections policy. We prioritize issues that could materially change a reader's estimate. We cannot promise a fixed response or repair deadline for every report.

Commercial boundaries

Advertising supports the website. An advertisement is not a lender recommendation or a guarantee that a product is suitable. We do not accept payment to change a calculation in an advertiser's favor. See our advertising disclosure and disclaimer.
