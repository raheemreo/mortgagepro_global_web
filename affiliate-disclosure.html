Commercial Independence & Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency statement regarding commercial affiliations, lender placement fees, and our mathematical objectivity.

Policy Date: September 2026 Governing Entity: REO Technologies Status: Strict Zero-Affiliate Architecture

🛡️ Summary: Zero Paid Lender Placements

MortgagePro Global operates with strict commercial independence. We do not receive referral fees, lead-generation bounty payments, or affiliate commissions from banks, mortgage originators, brokers, or financial institutions. All calculation algorithms are deterministic and uninfluenced by commercial partnerships.

1. Zero Affiliate Commission Policy

Unlike many online mortgage lead-generation portals, MortgagePro Global does not sell user contact details to loan originators, nor do we earn kickbacks when users apply for mortgages. Specifically:

  • We do not maintain affiliate relationships with commercial mortgage lenders.
  • We do not accept compensation to alter interest rate displays, ranking tables, or calculation default inputs.
  • We do not prioritize any financial institution or lending product over another.

2. Distinction Between Display Advertising & Commercial Endorsements

To fund server hosting, continuous formula engineering, and official rate registry monitoring, MortgagePro Global may display digital advertising delivered through certified ad networks (such as Google AdSense):

  • Independent Ad Delivery: Third-party advertisements served on our platform are managed through algorithmic ad auctions. We do not hand-pick advertisers, and ads displayed do not constitute an endorsement by MortgagePro Global or REO Technologies.
  • Zero Impact on Calculation Logic: Advertisements have zero connection to our client-side calculation engines. Your loan payment, total interest, and amortization numbers are produced strictly by mathematical formulas and official central bank rate inputs.

3. Mathematical Neutrality & Open Standards

Our commitment to users across the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, India, and Europe is uncompromising transparency:

  • Client-Side Processing: All loan calculations occur locally in your web browser. We never capture, transmit, or monetize your loan amounts, household income, or deposit values.
  • Primary Sources: Interest rate benchmarks and statutory tax brackets are cited directly from sovereign regulatory authorities (such as the Federal Reserve, Bank of Canada, Bank of England, RBA, RBNZ, RBI, and ECB). Review our full directory in the Data Sources Registry.
