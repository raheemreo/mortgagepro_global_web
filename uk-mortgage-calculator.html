England and Northern Ireland residential purchases only. First-time relief is unavailable for the entire purchase above £500,000; use standard rates on every band in that case. Non-residence can combine with additional-property rates. A buy-to-let label alone does not establish the surcharge. Companies, linked purchases, reliefs and lease rent need separate assessment. Compare the same repayment timeline

Enter the financed principal, the rate you want to test and the repayment years. A short fixed-rate deal can expire before the mortgage is repaid. Constant-rate lifetime interest is an illustration, not a prediction of every future refix.

At GBP 262,500, an assumed 4.48% and 25 years, the monthly repayment model gives GBP 1,456.08. Actual daily accrual, dates, financed product fees and lender rounding can change the disclosure figures. Keep council tax, insurance, service charges and maintenance outside the P&I number.

SDLT has a limited scope here

The root tool supports standard individual residential rates in England and Northern Ireland and an assumed eligible first-time-relief category. It does not add additional-property or non-UK-resident surcharges; use the expanded SDLT article tool or a conveyancer if those apply. Scotland's LBTT and Wales's LTT need different calculations.

Under HMRC's current bands, first-time relief has a GBP 500,000 price ceiling. A qualifying GBP 500,000 purchase gives GBP 10,000; exceeding the ceiling restores the standard calculation. Every purchaser's eligibility and the effective transaction date matter. A checkbox is not proof of relief.

A repayment estimate does not establish borrowing capacity

A lender assesses income, spending, commitments, credit and the property. There is no universal income multiple guaranteed to every applicant. The affordability guide distinguishes budget arithmetic from lender-flow regulation.

Interest-only and switching decisions

An interest-only payment does not reduce the starting balance. A later repayment phase may have fewer years to clear it. The repayment comparison illustrates that difference. For a switch, compare actual fees, early-repayment charges and remaining balances over a consistent horizon; a cashback headline or lower first payment is not the full cost comparison.

UK Mortgage Frequently Asked Questions

  • SDLT applies to relevant transactions in England and Northern Ireland. Scotland uses LBTT and Wales uses LTT. The jurisdiction, transaction date, property type and buyer category determine treatment; use the appropriate dedicated tool and confirm the actual case with the conveyancer rather than applying one UK-wide percentage.

  • First-time-buyer relief requires every relevant purchaser and transaction to meet the conditions. A checkbox models an assumed category; it does not prove entitlement. Price ceilings, additional-property treatment and residence rules can affect the result. Check HMRC's dated guidance for England and Northern Ireland and the separate national rules elsewhere.

  • LTV divides the mortgage principal by the relevant assessed property value. It is a descriptive ratio, not a complete underwriting decision. A changed valuation or financed fee can change it. The lender decides the valuation and product conditions; this site does not infer a rate discount from a chosen LTV band.

  • Interest-only payments do not reduce principal in the illustrated phase. The debt must still be settled, and a later repayment payment can be larger when fewer years remain. Check the lender's repayment-plan requirements and use the transition tool instead of treating the lower opening debit as full repayment.

  • The fixed deal period and the total repayment term can be different. A tracker follows the contract's named benchmark and margin; it does not necessarily equal the policy rate alone. Compare the actual reset and follow-on terms. No future offer or fixed-period extension is predicted here.

  • Remortgage comparisons should include the same comparison horizon, remaining balances, known fees, cashback obligations and early repayment charges. Extending the term can reduce the monthly payment while increasing interest. The dedicated quote comparison separates cash-flow savings from the cost of debt over that horizon.
