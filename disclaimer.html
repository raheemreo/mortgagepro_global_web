Financial Information Disclaimer

MortgagePro Global provides educational estimates, not loan offers, approval decisions or personal financial, legal or tax advice.

Updated October 25, 2026 · Publisher: REO Technologies

A mortgage estimate can help you compare assumptions. It cannot establish that a lender will approve the loan, that you qualify for a tax relief or that a property is a suitable purchase.

The scope of the website

MortgagePro Global is an educational calculation and publishing website. It is not a bank, mortgage lender, broker, insurance provider or regulated advisory service. A result does not create a lending commitment or a professional advisory relationship.

Why actual results can differ

Calculators use the rate, principal, period and other assumptions shown with them. Actual contracts can use different interest timing, fees, rounding, repayment dates, offsets and insurance treatment. Rates and underwriting rules can change before completion or during a loan's repayment period.

A monthly principal-and-interest payment excludes other ownership costs unless the tool expressly adds them. Taxes, insurance, service charges, maintenance and closing costs should be budgeted separately. Constant-rate or constant-cost lifetime totals are scenarios rather than forecasts.

Eligibility and local rules

Country, state, province, property use, borrower category and transaction date can affect tax, insurance and assistance programs. Selecting a relief or eligibility field does not prove that its conditions are met. Verify an actual case with the relevant lender, authority, conveyancer or qualified adviser.

A regulatory limit on a lender's portfolio is not necessarily an individual borrowing cap. Likewise, an exemption does not guarantee approval. Read each tool's scope and the primary references linked in its explanation.

Rates and commercial content

A central-bank policy rate is not a personal mortgage offer. A dated survey or historical benchmark does not guarantee current availability to a particular borrower. An advertisement is separate from a calculator result and is not endorsed as suitable for you.

Errors and decisions

We work to make explanations useful and to correct reported problems. We do not claim that every page has been independently reviewed by a licensed financial professional. Report an apparent error through the corrections policy, and verify figures before making a commitment.

This explanation does not remove any rights or obligations that applicable law does not allow to be excluded. See the terms and privacy policy for the website's other conditions and data practices.
