If no dialog appears

Availability depends on Google's published message configuration, your region and whether the provider can load. A browser or network blocker can also affect the control. We do not interpret an unavailable dialog as consent. Read our privacy-control guidance and cookie policy, or report the problem through Contact. Do not send financial records or identity documents.

Changing a choice

When the provider interface is available, it records the choices that apply to subsequent processing. Reopening the EU message pauses advertising requests while you reconsider. Changing a choice does not erase correspondence, downloaded files or information already held by another provider. Browser site-data controls and Google's own ad controls offer separate options explained in the policy.
